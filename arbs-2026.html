Taokron at ARBS 2026: show recap

We had Stand 149 at ARBS 2026, the Air Conditioning, Refrigeration and Building Services exhibition at the Melbourne Convention and Exhibition Centre, 5 to 7 May 2026. Thank you to everyone who came by.

Next Australian show: ARBS QLD 2027, Brisbane, 2 to 3 June 2027, Halls 3, Stand 122.

What was on the stand

ARBS is a shell-stand show, so we brought the range to the stand rather than the machines themselves:

  • The full machine range on the stand walls: spiral tubeformers, auto duct lines, TDF flange formers, lockformers, welders, cutting and flexible duct machines.
  • Real samples: spiral and flexible duct, TDF flanged rectangular duct and fittings, so visitors could check seams and flanges in their hands.
  • Machine videos on screen, printed catalogues and spec sheets.
  • Our team, for quotations, shop planning and questions about power supply, shipping and installation.

Who we met

Duct shops, mechanical contractors and existing customers from around Australia, plus visitors from overseas.

Want to see a machine running?

The best way now is a real shop. Our newest Australian install is Airtemp Canberra, a complete 8-machine duct shop commissioned in September 2026; the case study has photos and video from site. For round duct, the Kavanagh Industries case study shows an SBTF-1602 spiral tubeformer installed in Sydney in October 2026. We can also send videos of any machine in the range running, filmed for your coil and duct sizes.
