SBKJ Group at ISH Frankfurt 2027 — HVAC Ductwork Machinery Live Demonstrations

Meet the SBKJ engineering team at ISH Frankfurt 2027 at Messe Frankfurt, 15–19 March 2027 — the world’s largest trade show for HVAC, water, sanitation and renewable energy. Three live CE-marked, EN 1505/1506-compliant machines on the stand wired for 400V/50Hz European power, 30-minute private engineering consultations across the five show days, and indicative EUR landed-cost worksheets on the show floor.

Book a private booth meeting

Why visit the SBKJ stand at ISH Frankfurt 2027

ISH Frankfurt is the world’s largest trade show for HVAC, water, sanitation and renewable energy, with 200,000+ visitors and 2,400+ exhibitors across five days. For German, Austrian, Swiss, Dutch, Belgian and broader European fabricators evaluating new duct production equipment, ISH is the premium five-day window where the engineering team that designs the machines is on the floor running real coil through real machines — CE-marked, EN-compliant, 400V/50Hz European-spec demo units, not American 480V/60Hz units that don’t deploy in Europe without rewiring.

SBKJ exhibits at ISH Frankfurt and Mostra Convegno Expocomfort Milan on alternating years to maintain consistent European visibility for HVAC-equipment buyers. ISH 2027 follows MCE 2026 (March 2026); ISH 2029 will follow MCE 2028 (March 2028).

What’s on the stand

SBKJ runs three live demonstrations on a rolling 30-minute cycle across the five show days:

1. SBTF series spiral tubeformer

  • Diameter range: Phi 100 mm to Phi 800 mm (covering most EN 1506 commercial HVAC supply, return and exhaust specifications)
  • Coil: 137 mm slit galvanized steel coil, 0.5–1.0 mm thickness range
  • Cutting: Flying-shear cutting on the demo unit
  • Output: Up to 30 m/min depending on diameter and gauge

2. SBAL-V auto duct production line

  • Configuration: Coil-to-finished-rectangular-section in one continuous flow
  • Section size: Up to 1,500 mm wide, 0.6–1.5 mm gauge
  • Cycle time: Standard rectangular section in approximately 3 minutes
  • Control: Siemens S7-1500 PLC with HMI in German, English, Italian, French, Spanish
  • Pressure class: EN 1507 leakage class A, B, C and D

3. TDF flange former

  • Roll-forms TDF flange directly into the rectangular duct edge
  • EN 1507 Class C/D leakage compliant when paired with matched corner clip
  • Live forming on 30 cm samples; visitors take home a flanged offcut

About ISH Frankfurt 2027

ISH (Internationale Sanitär- und Heizungsmesse) is the largest HVAC, water and sanitation trade show in the world, run biennially by Messe Frankfurt at the Frankfurt fairgrounds. ISH 2027 is held from Monday 15 to Friday 19 March 2027. Registration is via ish.messefrankfurt.com. The show typically draws 200,000+ industry professionals from contracting, building services consulting, plumbing, renewable energy, refrigeration and equipment manufacturing across Europe, MENA and CIS markets.

Practical information

  • Venue: Messe Frankfurt, Ludwig-Erhard-Anlage 1, 60327 Frankfurt am Main, Germany
  • Closest train: Frankfurt-Messe S-Bahn station (S3, S4, S5, S6) directly at fairground
  • Closest airport: Frankfurt Main (FRA, 12 km direct rail link)
  • Hotels: Maritim Hotel Frankfurt (walking distance), Steigenberger Frankfurter Hof, Marriott Frankfurt
  • Show times: 09:00–18:00 Monday through Thursday, 09:00–17:00 Friday
  • Registration: Free for trade visitors via ish.messefrankfurt.com
