SBKJ Group at Chillventa 2026 — Hall 7, Stand 7-345

Meet the SBKJ engineering team at the world’s leading exhibition for refrigeration, AC & ventilation and heat pumps, Exhibition Centre Nuremberg, 13–15 October 2026. HVAC duct fabrication machinery — spiral tubeformers, auto duct production lines, TDF flange formers and lockformers — with engineer-led private consultations across all three show days at Hall 7, Stand 7-345.

Hosted by SBKJ Australia · Messezentrum Nürnberg, Hall 7, Stand 7-345 · 13–15 October 2026

Book a stand meeting   What’s on the stand

Why visit Stand 7-345

Ventilation is half of Chillventa’s title — and every ventilation system runs on ductwork. The contractors, system builders and AC equipment manufacturers walking Hall 7 are exactly the businesses that fabricate or buy rectangular and round duct, and the machinery behind that duct sets its cost per square metre for the next decade.

SBKJ brings the engineering team, not a sales team. Three days of real specification questions answered with real numbers: coil-to-output mapping for your gauge range, EN 1505/1506 dimensional compliance, EN 1507/12237 air-tightness classes, throughput modelling against your shift pattern, freight to your port, and installation supervision scope. If you currently buy duct in, the consultation includes a make-vs-buy worksheet against your annual volume.

What’s on the stand

SBKJ presents its core duct fabrication range at Hall 7, Stand 7-345. Email sales@sbkjduct.com to receive the stand programme as soon as it is finalised.

Spiral tubeformers (SBTF series)

  • Round duct production from slit galvanised, stainless or aluminium coil.
  • Diameter coverage across the SBTF range from Φ80 mm to Φ2,000 mm — commercial supply air through large industrial extract.
  • Flying-shear and saw cutting options; lockseam construction meeting EN 12237 and AS/NZS 4254 leakage classes.
  • Full specifications: spiral duct machinery range.

Auto duct production lines (SBAL series)

  • Coil-to-finished-rectangular-section in one continuous flow: decoiler, levelling, notch and corner punch, lockformer, folding, TDF flange integration.
  • Line configurations from entry-level SBAL-II to the flagship SBAL-V U-shape line — one operator, 18 m/min line speed.
  • Cut-list import from Revit MEP and CAMduct supported — bring a sample fabrication file to the stand.
  • Full specifications: auto duct production lines.

TDF flange formers and lockformers

  • Roll-formed TDF flange directly into the duct edge — eliminates welded angle-iron flanges and their 8–12 kg per joint.
  • Pittsburgh lockformers and seam closers for longitudinal seams across 0.5–2.0 mm galvanised.
  • Full specifications: TDF and lockformer range.

Private engineering consultations

SBKJ runs 30-minute private consultations at Stand 7-345 throughout the three show days, led by an SBKJ engineer with a structured agenda:

  1. Project brief (5 min) — coil spec, throughput target, market and standards.
  2. Machine match (10 min) — specific SBKJ model recommendation, footprint, utility requirements.
  3. Indicative landed cost (10 min) — machine price, freight to your port, installation supervision days, training scope.
  4. Next steps (5 min) — FAT timeline, machine demonstration options, contract milestones.

Consultations are free and non-binding. Book a slot in advance — morning slots fill first.

Pre-show preparation — what to bring

  1. Your current coil specification. Width, thickness, material, supplier mill cert if available.
  2. Your target throughput. Square metres of duct per shift, linear metres per hour, or sections per day.
  3. Your standards reference. EN 1505/1506 dimensions and EN 1507/12237 air-tightness for Europe; DW/144 for the UK; SMACNA for North American export; AS/NZS 4254 for Australia and New Zealand.

Optional but useful: a sample Revit MEP or CAMduct cut list, a workshop floor plan in PDF, and your current bottleneck description. If you buy duct in today, bring last year’s purchased volume — the make-vs-buy worksheet needs one number.

About Chillventa 2026

Chillventa is the world’s leading exhibition for refrigeration, air conditioning & ventilation and heat pump technology, held at the Exhibition Centre Nuremberg (Messezentrum Nürnberg), Germany, from Tuesday 13 to Thursday 15 October 2026, organised by NürnbergMesse. The show spans the complete cold and air chain — components, systems, applications and digitalisation — preceded by the Chillventa CONGRESS on 12 October.

Visitor information and tickets: chillventa.de.

Frequently asked questions

Which languages does the SBKJ stand team speak?

English and Mandarin-speaking engineers are on the stand across all three days. German, French, Spanish, Arabic, Turkish, Russian or Indonesian language support for a private consultation can be arranged with 48 hours’ notice — email sales@sbkjduct.com.

Can I get pricing at the stand?

Yes. SBKJ engineers issue indicative pricing for standard machine configurations during your consultation, based on your coil specification, throughput requirement and destination. Final binding quotations including freight, insurance and installation supervision follow within 5 business days of the show.

What happens after a stand consultation?

Within 5 business days, the SBKJ engineer who hosted your consultation sends a written technical clarification, an indicative landed-cost worksheet for your destination port, and a draft project timeline. The next step is typically a Factory Acceptance Test booking against your nominated coil specification.

Can I see SBKJ at EuroBLECH the following week instead?

Yes — SBKJ exhibits at EuroBLECH 2026 in Hanover (Hall 015, Stand B02, 20–23 October), five days after Chillventa closes. If your focus is sheet metal fabrication technology rather than HVACR systems, EuroBLECH may suit your visit better; the same engineering team staffs both stands.

Does SBKJ exhibit at other shows?

Yes. SBKJ exhibited at ARBS 2026 in Sydney (May 2026), exhibits at EuroBLECH 2026 in Hanover (20–23 October 2026) and ISK-SODEX Istanbul 2027 (Hall 8, Stand E12, 19–22 October 2027); Mega Clima Algeria 2026 in Algiers (16–19 November 2026) follows a month after the German shows, and AHR Expo 2027 in Chicago (25–27 January 2027) is planned. Stand details are published here as each show is confirmed.
