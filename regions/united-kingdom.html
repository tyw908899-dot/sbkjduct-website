Taokron in the UK — market overview

The UK HVAC duct equipment market is anchored by four structural drivers: commercial real estate fitout (London City + Canary Wharf + West End office cycle), data centre hyperscale build-out across the London Docklands + Slough Trading Estate + Manchester + Cambridge corridor, the AUKUS UK pillar at BAE Systems Submarines Barrow-in-Furness with the SSN-AUKUS programme alongside Australian shipbuilding, and the post-Brexit infrastructure programme (HS2 high-speed rail, nuclear new-build at Hinkley Point C and Sizewell C, Heathrow third-runway debate).

Taokron machinery has been installed across British fabrication shops since the early 2010s, predominantly SBAL-V and SBAL-III auto duct lines for commercial real estate fitouts and data centre packages, SBTF spiral tubeformers for circular duct supply, and TDF flange formers for tight DW/144 leakage compliance. UK labour cost (GBP 50-90/hr loaded for sheet metal worker) makes automation attractive at moderate-volume thresholds.

UK compliance and standards

  • DW/144 Specification for Sheet Metal Ductwork (BESA): the dominant UK ductwork specification, gauge tables, joint types, pressure classes (low, medium, high), leakage classes A/B/C/D.
  • DW/154 Specification for Plastic Ductwork (BESA): plastic duct construction.
  • DW/172 Specification for Kitchen Ventilation (BESA): commercial kitchen exhaust.
  • CIBSE Guide A Environmental Design: HVAC design loads and air movement.
  • CIBSE TM52 Limits of Thermal Comfort.
  • BS EN 1505/1506: rectangular and circular sheet metal duct dimensions and pressure classes (EU/UK shared).
  • BS EN 1507/12237: leakage testing of rectangular and circular ducts.
  • BS 5588 Part 6: fire precautions in atria.
  • Building Regulations Part F: ventilation requirements (post-2022 revision with enhanced IAQ focus).
  • Building Regulations Part L: energy efficiency and SAP/SBEM calculation.
  • BIM Level 2 mandatory on UK government projects per PAS 1192-2:2013, with COBie data exchange and IFC file formats.
  • UKCA marking: post-Brexit conformity assessment (CE certification still accepted indefinitely as of 2024).

Customs and post-Brexit considerations

  • UK Global Tariff (UKGT) MFN duty: 0% on HS 8479.89 (UK simplified the post-Brexit tariff schedule in 2021).
  • UK VAT 20%: Reclaimable for VAT-registered importers. EORI registration required.
  • UK CDS (Customs Declaration Service): Mandatory for UK imports since 2022.
  • Northern Ireland: Imports via Windsor Framework with EU customs treatment - requires EU EORI and may require CE certification.
  • UKCA marking acceptance: CE certification still accepted indefinitely as of 2024 UK government decision; UKCA marking only mandatory if specifically required by individual UK regulation.
