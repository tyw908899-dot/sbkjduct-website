AS/NZS 4254 and Australian construction standards

HVAC ductwork in Australia is governed by AS/NZS 4254.1 (low-pressure) and AS/NZS 4254.2 (medium and high-pressure) duct construction standards, which set minimum sheet gauges, reinforcement spacing, joint sealing classes and air-leakage limits. Taokron machines have been used by Australian fabricators on AS/NZS 4254-compliant projects for over a decade. The most common requirement we hear from Sydney and Melbourne contractors is tight TDF flange seam control for medium-pressure work — this is a function of forming-station tolerance on the duct line, not just operator skill, and Taokron's SBAL-V auto duct line is specified accordingly with ±0.2 mm flange-bend tolerance as standard.

Power supply, voltage and electrical compliance

Australian sites run 415V three-phase / 50Hz as standard for industrial equipment, which matches Taokron's default European spec — no transformer or frequency conversion required. Taokron machines for Australia ship with electrical cabinets pre-wired to 415V/50Hz with Schneider, Siemens or Mitsubishi components compatible with local electrical compliance under AS/NZS 3000 (the Wiring Rules). Optional upgrades include AS/NZS-stamped emergency-stop circuits and additional EMC filtering for sites with strict electromagnetic interference limits.
