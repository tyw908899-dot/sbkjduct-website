SBKJ in Japan and South Korea — market overview

The combined Japan-Korea HVAC duct equipment market is anchored by two structural drivers that have made these economies strategic for SBKJ exports: the semiconductor capex super-cycle (Samsung Pyeongtaek P3-P5, SK hynix Icheon M16, TSMC Kumamoto, Rapidus Hokkaido) and the K-Battery Belt expansion (LG Energy Solution, Samsung SDI, SK On). Each fab and gigafactory carries an HVAC duct package in the USD 30-200 million range, driving demand for high-volume stainless-capable duct fabrication equipment in mechanical contractor workshops across both countries.

SBKJ machinery has been installed at Japanese and Korean fabricators since the mid-2010s — predominantly SBAL-V auto duct lines in stainless variants for semiconductor cleanroom and battery dry room work, SBTF spiral tubeformers for general industrial and commercial ventilation, and TDF flange formers for tight SMACNA Class A leakage compliance.

Compliance and standards

  • Japan: SHASE-S 005 (Society of Heating, Air-conditioning and Sanitary Engineers) standards, JIS A 4009 (HVAC duct construction), JIS G 3302 galvanized steel, JIS G 4304/4305 stainless steel.
  • Korea: KS B 6311 (HVAC duct), KS B 6361 (sheet metal duct construction), KICT (Korea Institute of Civil Engineering and Building Technology) standards.
  • Both: SMACNA HVAC Duct Construction Standards 4th Edition referenced for tier-1 commercial, semiconductor cleanroom and battery dry room. EN 1505/1506/1507 referenced for European-spec data centre and pharmaceutical projects. ISO 14644-1 cleanroom classifications.
  • Energy efficiency: Building Energy Efficiency Act (Japan), Korea Green Building Certification.
  • Electrical: Japan PSE marking and JET compliance; Korea KCS (Korea Certification Standards) for panel components.

Logistics and shipping

  • Japan ports: Yokohama (Tokyo gateway) 12-18 days from Melbourne; Nagoya (industrial heartland) 14-20 days; Osaka and Kobe (Western Japan) 14-20 days; Hakata Fukuoka (Kyushu, gateway for TSMC Kumamoto) 16-22 days; Sendai (Tohoku) 18-24 days.
  • Korea ports: Busan (dominant Korean port, ~75% of imports) 16-22 days; Incheon (Seoul gateway) 18-24 days; Pyeongtaek (Samsung semiconductor logistics) 16-22 days; Gwangyang (petrochemical and southern Korea) 17-23 days; Ulsan (Hyundai) 16-22 days.

Standard shipping is 40-foot high-cube container; larger machines on flat-rack via Yokohama or Busan. Both countries have efficient port-to-rail and port-to-truck logistics networks — inland transit from Yokohama to Tokyo industrial parks is 1 day; Busan to Seoul is 1-2 days.

Customs and free trade agreements

  • Japan-Australia Economic Partnership Agreement (JAEPA): In force since January 2015. HVAC duct forming machinery under HS 8479.89 typically attracts 0% MFN duty in Japan with JAEPA preferential treatment confirmed via Form-JP Certificate of Origin. Japanese consumption tax 10% reclaimable.
  • Korea-Australia Free Trade Agreement (KAFTA): In force since December 2014. HVAC duct machinery under HS 8479.89 typically at 0% under KAFTA via Certificate of Origin. Korean VAT 10% reclaimable.
  • SBKJ supplies the relevant Certificate of Origin with each shipment to support preferential duty claims.
