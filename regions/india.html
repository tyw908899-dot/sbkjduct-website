Taokron in India — market overview

The Indian HVAC duct equipment market is the fastest-growing in Asia-Pacific, structurally driven by four forces: the Production Linked Incentive (PLI) scheme attracting Apple/Foxconn/Tata/Wistron iPhone assembly and Micron semiconductor packaging into Tamil Nadu, Karnataka and Gujarat; the hyperscale data centre wave concentrated in Mumbai, NCR, Hyderabad and Bangalore; pharmaceutical and vaccine manufacturing capacity expansion (post-COVID Strategic Investment in Pharma); and the commercial real estate cycle anchored by Bangalore-Hyderabad-Chennai office demand from the Global Capability Centre (GCC) growth.

Taokron machinery is installed across Indian fabrication shops since the early 2010s — predominantly SBAL-III and SBAL-V auto duct lines for commercial and data centre projects, SBTF spiral tubeformers for industrial ventilation, and TDF flange formers for tight-leakage SMACNA-spec data centre work. Total Indian HVAC duct equipment market is approximately INR 30,000-45,000 crore (USD 3.6-5.4 billion) annually with strong year-on-year growth.

Compliance and standards

  • National Building Code 2016 Volume 2 covers HVAC general requirements.
  • Energy Conservation Building Code (ECBC) 2017 covers energy efficiency including duct insulation R-values by climate zone (5 zones across India).
  • IS 277 galvanized sheet steel; IS 5577 sheet metal duct construction.
  • SMACNA HVAC Duct Construction Standards 4th Edition or EN 1505 referenced for detailed tier-1 commercial, data centre, semiconductor and hospital projects.
  • ISHRAE Handbook (Indian Society of Heating, Refrigerating and Air Conditioning Engineers) the dominant local design reference.
  • IS 4047 cable colour coding; IS 13947 panel components; IS 8623 enclosure ingress protection.
  • PESO certification required for hazardous area work (chemical, fertiliser, refining).

  • Southern India: Chennai and Ennore (Tamil Nadu, also gateway for AP and Karnataka) 16-22 days direct.
  • Eastern India: Vishakhapatnam (AP and central India) 18-24 days; Krishnapatnam (AP) 18-24 days; Kolkata Haldia (Eastern India and Northeast) 22-28 days.

Standard shipping is 40-foot high-cube container for machines under 25 tonnes; larger machines on flat-rack via Nhava Sheva or Chennai. Buyers can choose CIF (Taokron handles freight and marine insurance to nominated port) or DDP for Mumbai/Pune/Bangalore/Chennai (Taokron handles door-to-door including Indian customs entry).

Customs and import duties

For HVAC duct forming machinery under HS 8479.89, standard Indian customs entry is basic customs duty 7.5%, IGST 18% (reclaimable for GST-registered importers) and social welfare surcharge 10% on the customs duty. Duty treatment depends on the HS classification and the country of origin of the goods; confirm the current rate and any applicable duties with your own Indian customs broker before importing.
