Taokron in Europe — market overview

The European HVAC duct equipment market is the second-largest in the world after North America, anchored by three structural drivers: the German Energiewende and Gebaude-Energie-Gesetz (GEG) retrofit programme, the EU Energy Performance of Buildings Directive (EPBD) recast driving deep retrofits across the bloc, and the post-Brexit UK regulatory divergence with UKCA marking running parallel to CE. Combined with hyperscale data centre construction in Frankfurt, Amsterdam, Dublin, Paris and Stockholm, plus pharmaceutical and semiconductor capex in Germany, France and Ireland, the region represents a EUR 8–10 billion annual HVAC duct equipment opportunity.

Taokron machinery has been installed across European fabrication shops since the early 2010s — predominantly SBAL-V and SBAL-III auto duct lines for commercial real estate fitouts and data centre projects, SBTF spiral tubeformers for ventilation and round duct supply, and TDF flange formers for tight EN 1507 Class C/D leakage compliance.

European compliance and standards

Taokron machinery produces ductwork compatible with the dominant European standards:

  • EN 1505 (rectangular sheet metal ducts — dimensions and pressure classes) and EN 1506 (circular sheet metal ducts) — the EU baseline for HVAC duct construction.
  • EN 1507 (rectangular ducts — air leakage testing) and EN 12237 (circular ducts — leakage testing) — pressure classes A, B, C, D for leakage.
  • EN 13779 (ventilation for non-residential buildings) and EN 16798 (energy performance of buildings ventilation) — design requirements.
  • VDI 6022 (Germany — hygiene requirements for ventilation systems) including filter classes, duct cleanliness and inspection.
  • DW/144 (UK — specification for sheet metal ductwork from BESA/HVCA).
  • NF DTU 68 (France — ductwork installation), NF EN series for product standards.
  • GEG / Gebaeudeenergiegesetz (Germany — building energy act) driving Energiewende retrofit.
  • RT 2020 / RE 2020 (France — thermal regulation post-2022) for new construction energy targets.
  • NCM (UK — National Calculation Methodology) for SAP and SBEM building energy calculation.
  • UKCA marking for the UK market (replacing CE for UK-only products).

CE Machinery Directive and machine specifications

Taokron machinery destined for European installation is CE certified under Machinery Directive 2006/42/EC at the factory:

  • Risk assessment: EN ISO 12100 hazard analysis with documented mitigation.
  • Safety circuits: EN ISO 13849-1 Performance Level d (PLd) minimum on emergency stop, light curtains and interlocked guarding.
  • Guarding: EN ISO 14120 fixed and movable guards on all moving parts; interlocked access doors per EN ISO 14119.
  • Electrical safety: EN 60204-1 industrial machinery electrical equipment standard.
  • EMC: EN 61000-6-2 immunity and EN 61000-6-4 emissions for industrial environments.
  • Power: 400V three-phase 50Hz with TN-S earthing as standard; 230V single-phase auxiliary.
  • Enclosures: IP54 panel enclosures with Siemens or Schneider Electric components.
  • Documentation: Declaration of Conformity, technical file summary, residual risk list, operator and maintenance manuals in your country language, certificate of origin.

Logistics and shipping

Taokron ships from Melbourne to all major European ports. Typical port-to-port lead times via Suez Canal:

  • North Sea: Hamburg, Bremerhaven 35-42 days; Rotterdam, Antwerp 33-40 days; Felixstowe, Southampton 35-42 days.
  • Mediterranean: Genoa, La Spezia 28-35 days; Marseille, Fos 28-35 days; Valencia, Algeciras 28-35 days; Trieste 30-37 days.
  • Atlantic: Le Havre 35-42 days; Lisbon 32-38 days; Cork (Ireland) 38-45 days.
  • Baltic and Nordic: Gdansk 38-45 days; Gothenburg, Aarhus, Helsinki 40-48 days via North Sea feeder.

Standard shipping is 40-foot high-cube container; larger machines on 40-foot flat-rack or break-bulk through Antwerp or Rotterdam. CIF and DDP Incoterms supported; first-time European buyers typically choose CIF with their own customs broker handling EU customs entry, T1 transit document and VAT clearance.

Customs and import documentation

  • TARIC code: 8479 89 97 for most Taokron duct machinery; 8462 49 for some forming machines.
  • EU MFN duty: 1.7% on the CIF value for HS 8479.89 under the standard Common External Tariff.
  • Import VAT: Destination-country rate (DE 19%, FR 20%, IT 22%, ES 21%, NL 21%, BE 21%, IE 23%, NO 25%, PL 23%, CZ 21%, etc.) reclaimable for VAT-registered importers.
  • UK Global Tariff (post-Brexit): 0% for HS 8479.89.97 with UK VAT 20%.
  • Documentation pack: Commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading, generic certificate of origin, Declaration of Conformity, REACH and RoHS conformity statements.
  • EORI number: Importer must have a valid EORI number registered in the EU member state of customs entry.

Installation supervision in Europe

Every Taokron machine purchase includes 5-10 days of installation supervision and operator training by a Taokron engineer at your shop. Engineers travel on Schengen business visitor visas (where applicable; Australian passport holders get visa-free 90-day Schengen access) or on UK business visitor entry. English is the standard working language for commissioning; local-language interpreter support is arranged with 4 weeks notice for German, French, Italian, Spanish or Polish operator training where requested.

Taokron engineers carry SCC (Safety Certificate Contractors) cards as standard for European industrial site access. Country-specific contractor inductions (e.g. SCCP, VCA in Netherlands) are arranged in advance with the buyer.

Does Taokron have CE Notified Body involvement?

For machinery within the standard scope of Machinery Directive 2006/42/EC Annex IV, manufacturers may self-certify with internal production control and a CE certification based on harmonised standards conformity. Taokron machinery uses harmonised standards (EN ISO 12100, EN ISO 13849-1, EN 60204-1) for self-certification; a Notified Body assessment is not required for the standard SBAL-V and SBTF families. For modified or special-configuration machines, Taokron engages a EU-based Notified Body where required.
