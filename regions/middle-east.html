Climate and material considerations for the Gulf

The Middle East presents three machinery selection factors that differ from temperate markets. Ambient heat in inland Saudi Arabia and the UAE regularly exceeds 45°C in summer; SBKJ machines ship with extended-temperature electrical cabinets and reinforced cooling fans on the servo drives as standard for GCC orders. Coastal humidity and salt air in Dubai, Doha, Jeddah and Muscat accelerate corrosion of unprotected steel — for these locations we recommend G115 galvanized coil and stainless 316 for accessory components rather than the lower-spec G60 used in dry inland projects. Sand and dust ingress shortens bearing life in unsealed mechanical assemblies; SBKJ duct lines destined for the GCC use IP54-rated motors and fully enclosed servo gearboxes.

Project profile: typical Middle East HVAC contractor

Most SBKJ Middle East customers fall into one of three buyer profiles. The first is the large mechanical contractor running tier-one commercial projects (airports, malls, hospitals, mixed-use towers) — these buyers usually specify a complete SBAL-V auto duct line plus a Φ100–1600 mm spiral tubeformer to cover both rectangular and round duct in a single shop. The second profile is the specialist duct fabricator supplying multiple contractors — they tend to add gore-lockers, elbow makers and fitting equipment to extend their accessory range. The third is the vertically integrated developer or facility owner bringing duct fabrication in-house for ongoing maintenance and refit work — typically a single SBAL-III or SBTF-1500 is enough for their volume.

Voltage, frequency and electrical conformity

Most of the GCC runs on 380 V or 400 V three-phase 50 Hz, which matches the SBKJ standard machine build — no transformer is required. Saudi Arabia is the major exception, with 220 V single-phase and 380 V three-phase at 60 Hz on most modern industrial connections. SBKJ ships 60 Hz drive packages for Saudi orders at no extra cost when the customer specifies it on the original enquiry. All machines for the Middle East are supplied with English-language wiring diagrams, an Arabic operator quick-reference card, and the SASO conformity documentation needed for customs clearance in Saudi Arabia.

Frequently asked questions for Middle East buyers

Do SBKJ machines run on 60 Hz Saudi power?

Yes. SBKJ ships 60 Hz drive packages for Saudi Arabia at no extra cost when the customer specifies the local supply on the original enquiry. Other GCC countries run on 50 Hz which is the SBKJ standard build.

What documents do I need to clear SBKJ machinery through GCC customs?

Standard documentation is the commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, CE declaration of conformity, and (for Saudi Arabia) the SASO conformity certificate. SBKJ supplies all of these as standard.

Can SBKJ provide engineers who speak Arabic?

SBKJ commissioning engineers work in English, which is the standard language for all GCC industrial sites. For projects with Arabic-only operator teams, SBKJ partners with local interpreters during the on-site training week.

How long does delivery take to the Gulf?

Total order-to-delivery is approximately 4 months for a single machine and 6–8 months for a complete auto duct line, including 45–60 days build time and 22–35 days sea freight from Melbourne or Sydney to Jebel Ali, Dammam, Hamad Port, Sohar or Salalah.
