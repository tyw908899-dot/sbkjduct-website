Battery gigafactory HVAC at a glance

Battery gigafactories are among the most demanding industrial HVAC environments in the world. Cell assembly clean dry rooms operate at dewpoints of -40°C to -60°C (typical commercial cold storage operates at -25°C dewpoint, by comparison) with 30-60 air changes per hour, simultaneously managing high latent loads from electrode coating, NMP solvent vapour from the coating oven exhaust, formation and aging heat load, and the catastrophic risk of lithium thermal runaway. The HVAC duct package alone for a 40 GWh-class gigafactory is USD 30-80 million.

The current global wave of battery investment is unprecedented: 240+ gigafactories announced or under construction globally as of 2026, with combined capacity over 7,500 GWh by 2030 (BloombergNEF, IEA). Each project requires stainless and galvanised duct fabrication at scale. Taokron SBAL-V stainless variant is the standard cell assembly dry room duct fabrication line; SBAL-V galvanised handles formation/aging and pack assembly.

Process areas and HVAC requirements

  • Electrode coating: NMP-laden exhaust at 100-200°C, condenser-coil integration with duct system, polyethylene-lined steel or PVDF construction.
  • Calendering, slitting, notching, stacking, can welding: Cell assembly clean dry room at -40 to -60°C dewpoint, ISO 7-8 cleanliness, 304L electropolished stainless supply duct.
  • Electrolyte filling: 20-40 ACH dedicated negative pressure for VOC capture (DMC, EMC, dimethyl carbonate).
  • Formation and aging: 15-25 ACH, high heat load from formation cycle, smoke and gas detection in HVAC for thermal runaway early warning.
  • Pack assembly: 10-15 ACH ISO 8-9, galvanised G90 supply, lower cleanliness than cell assembly.
  • Quality control and laboratory: ISO 7 cleanroom, dedicated AHU, vibration isolation if vibration-sensitive testing.

Taokron machinery for battery gigafactory projects

  • SBAL-V auto duct line (stainless variant): 304L stainless rectangular duct for cell assembly clean dry rooms, TDF flange integration with stainless corner clips, SMACNA Class A or EN 1507 Class D leakage compliant.
  • SBAL-V auto duct line (galvanised variant): G90 galvanised rectangular duct for formation, aging and pack assembly areas.
  • SBTF spiral tubeformer (stainless): Round duct in 304L for cell assembly return air and supply plenum.
  • SBTF spiral tubeformer (galvanised): Round duct for formation/aging return and pack assembly.
  • Orbital TIG welding station: Closed-loop seam welding for stainless cell assembly supply requiring full-penetration welds.
  • Pickle and passivate equipment: ASTM A380/A967 surface treatment for stainless duct.

Reference projects

The current wave of gigafactory construction includes Tesla Berlin (50 GWh), Tesla Texas (100 GWh combined with Tesla Reno via Panasonic JV), LG Energy Solution Wroclaw (Europe's largest at 100+ GWh), LG Energy Solution Holland Michigan (with GM Ultium JV), Samsung SDI Goed Hungary (40+ GWh, expanding), SK On Georgia (combined Hyundai-Kia JV plus standalone), Panasonic Kansas (50 GWh, the new Wisconsin-area expansion), Northvolt Skelleftea Sweden (60 GWh once fully built), ACC Douvrin France (40 GWh), Verkor Dunkerque France (16 GWh, expanding), Stellantis-LGES Windsor Ontario (45 GWh), and Australian projects Energy Renaissance Tomago NSW (5 GWh) and Recharge Industries Geelong VIC (planned 30 GWh).

Each of these projects carries a HVAC duct package of USD 30-80 million depending on capacity and chemistry. Taokron machinery is installed at the regional mechanical fabricators supplying these projects rather than at the gigafactory itself — the battery cell maker does not manufacture its own ductwork.
