Stadium HVAC at a glance

Stadiums and sports venues are uniquely demanding HVAC environments: massive scale (60,000-100,000 seats), peak event loads vs idle days, retractable roofs requiring dual-mode HVAC, mixed-use spaces (concourse, premium suite, locker room, kitchen, BOH), and event-driven occupancy patterns. The HVAC duct package alone for a major stadium is USD 50-150 million.

The current stadium construction wave is unprecedented. Brisbane 2032 Olympic infrastructure (AUD 7+ billion total programme), FIFA World Cup 2026 (US/Canada/Mexico co-hosting), 2030 (Spain/Portugal/Morocco), 2034 (Saudi Arabia), Olympic Games Paris 2024 legacy and Milan-Cortina Winter 2026, LA 2028 venue construction, plus continuous Australian AFL/NRL stadium upgrades. Combined, multi-billion-dollar HVAC duct demand globally through 2034.

Stadium HVAC zones and requirements

  • Bowl (spectator zone): High-volume low-velocity supply, under-seat displacement or perimeter slot diffusers, smoke management exhaust per NFPA 92.
  • Concourse and circulation: High-volume air handling, peak-hour demand-controlled ventilation, smoke management for assembly occupancy.
  • Premium suite: Dedicated AHU per suite, NC-25/NC-30 acoustic, VAV terminals for occupant control.
  • Locker room and player facility: High humidity, frequent shower-cycle dehumidification, rapid recovery between matches.
  • Catering kitchen: NFPA 96 commercial cooking, grease ducts (Schedule 10 black steel welded), Type I/II hood exhaust.
  • Retractable roof: Dual-mode HVAC (open-roof natural / closed-roof mechanical), roof state interlock.
  • Broadcast area: Tight temperature control for camera and electronics, low NC for audio quality.
  • BOH operations: Standard commercial HVAC for offices, security, medical bay.

SBKJ machinery for stadium projects

  • SBAL-V auto duct production line (galvanised G90): Standard for bowl rectangular supply, concourse rectangular, premium suite supply, BOH HVAC.
  • SBTF spiral tubeformer: Round duct for concourse, atrium, retail, suite supply branches.
  • TDF flange former: Tight-leakage SMACNA Class A flange for smoke management ducts and pressurised areas.

  • Stainless variant: 304L stainless for locker room dehumidification ducts (corrosion resistance to chlorinated atmosphere from showers/pools).
