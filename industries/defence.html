Defence HVAC at a glance

The Australian defence industrial undertaking is in unprecedented expansion. AUKUS Pillar 1 + Pillar 2 (signed September 2021) commits USD 245+ billion through 2055 covering nuclear-powered conventionally-armed submarine acquisition (Virginia-class US-built initially, then Australian-built SSN-AUKUS) and advanced capabilities cooperation across AI, quantum, hypersonics, cyber, electronic warfare, undersea systems and autonomous systems. The Henderson WA shipbuilding precinct (Civmec, Austal, Luerssen Australia, BAE Systems Australia Henderson) and Adelaide SA Osborne (ASC, BAE Systems Australia Adelaide) host the build sites.

HVAC scope is multi-billion-dollar across submarine accommodation, surface vessel HVAC, shipyard hall HVAC, and adjacent support facility HVAC. Taokron machinery is installed at the regional mechanical fabricators supplying these defence projects rather than at the shipyard or base directly.

Australian defence facility programmes

Active facility programmes driving HVAC duct demand:

  • AUKUS submarine programme: Build sites at Henderson WA + Osborne SA, plus support facility expansion at HMAS Stirling Rockingham WA (future SSN-AUKUS basing).
  • RAAF airbases: Williamtown NSW (F-35 base), Amberley QLD (F/A-18, P-8, KC-30), Tindal NT (F-35 forward base, US Marine rotational), Pearce WA (training), Edinburgh SA (P-8, EA-18G), Williams VIC, Richmond NSW.
  • RAN naval bases: Garden Island Sydney (fleet base east), HMAS Stirling Rockingham WA (fleet base west), HMAS Coonawarra Darwin (northern naval).
  • Army facilities: Townsville QLD (3rd Brigade, ADF Special Operations), Holsworthy NSW, Puckapunyal VIC, Singleton NSW, Robertson Barracks Darwin (1st Brigade).
  • Training establishments: Royal Military College Duntroon, Australian Defence Force Academy (ADFA) Canberra, HMAS Cerberus Western Port, RAAF College Wagga.
  • Defence Science: DSTG (Defence Science and Technology Group) Edinburgh SA, Fishermans Bend VIC.

HVAC requirements unique to defence

  • Hardened facility HVAC: Blast-resistant ducts in command bunkers per USACE UFC 3-340-02, structurally hardened against nearby explosions.
  • CBRN collective protection: Carbon + HEPA filter banks for chemical-biological-radiological-nuclear protection, +25-50 Pa positive overpressure to exclude contaminated atmosphere.
  • EMP shielding: Faraday cage construction with HVAC ducts as conduits, EMP-rated dampers, copper or steel mesh penetrations (waveguide-below-cutoff WBC for ductwork through EMP barrier).
  • TEMPEST emanation control: HVAC ducts can act as RF antennas for compromising emanations from classified electronics; separate HVAC zoning between classified and unclassified spaces.
  • Shock and vibration: Naval ducts must withstand shock loading per MIL-S-901D, vibration tolerance per MIL-STD-167.
  • Ammunition magazine and fuel depot HVAC: ATEX/IECEx zone classification per AS/NZS 60079, NFPA 30/495, DDESB safety distances.

Henderson WA shipbuilding precinct context

Henderson WA hosts the largest concentration of Australian shipbuilding capacity:

  • Civmec: Defence Capability Manual partner, modular shipbuilding, mechanical engineering for AUKUS sub-assemblies.
  • Austal: Offshore patrol vessel for RAN, US Navy Independence-class LCS supplier, Cape-class patrol boats for Australian Border Force.
  • Luerssen Australia: Arafura-class offshore patrol vessels for RAN.
  • BAE Systems Australia Henderson: Hunter-class frigate construction.
  • Western Australian Defence Industry hub: Planned expansion for AUKUS submarine sub-module work.

Each shipbuilder operates 100,000+ m³ assembly halls with major HVAC scope: hall climate control, paint booth integration (per automotive paint booth guide adapted to marine), machine shop ventilation, office building HVAC. Taokron machinery at the regional mechanical fabricators is the standard fabrication equipment.

Taokron machinery scope at defence projects

  • SBAL-V auto duct line (316L stainless variant): Naval shipbuilding accommodation, surface ship engine room ventilation, classified facility HVAC.
  • SBAL-V auto duct line (galvanised): General base barracks, admin building, mess hall, electrical room, training facility HVAC.
  • SBAL-V (heavy-gauge naval variant): 1.5-2.0 mm gauge for shock-rated zones requiring MIL-S-901D compliance.
  • SBTF spiral tubeformer (316L stainless): Round duct for marine atmosphere applications.
  • Orbital TIG welding station: Welded longitudinal seam for shock-rated naval ducts.
  • TDF flange former (galvanised + stainless): Tight-leakage SMACNA Class A or EN 1507 Class D flange for CBRN-protected pressurised compartment supply.
  • Out of standard scope: Heavy-gauge welded blast-resistant ducts (welded fabrication shops with submerged-arc capability), TEMPEST RF-shielded duct fabrication (specialist contractors).
