What this category is

A focused category definition for faster top-level qualification.

Elbow and fittings machines are specialized equipment used to produce segmented elbows and related connection parts, then weld or shape them for final assembly. They are essential when fitting quality and repeatability affect installation outcomes.

When to choose this

Use these signals to decide if this category fits your workflow.

  • You produce round duct systems that require recurring elbow and fitting batches.
  • You need better consistency than manual fitting fabrication can deliver.
  • You want dedicated equipment for elbow forming, welding, and shaping tasks.
  • Your projects require repeatable fitting geometry for easier site installation.
  • You are balancing fitting output with upstream duct body production.
  • You need a process that supports quality checks before dispatch.

Typical production workflow

A clear step sequence from raw material to quality release.

  1. Material preparation: cut/prepare segments according to fitting design.
  2. Elbow/fitting forming: gorelocker and shaping equipment produce geometry.
  3. Joining and welding: elbow welder or stitch welding steps complete structural joining.
  4. Finishing operations: edge treatment and dimensional adjustment as required.
  5. Quality control: verify fit-up quality, weld condition, and dimensional consistency before shipment or installation.

Key buying criteria

Prioritize these checkpoints before model shortlisting and layout lock-in.

  • Capacity/output: Define target shift output and identify the real bottleneck step before choosing machine configuration.
  • Labor/automation: Match automation level to labor availability, operator skill structure, and consistency requirements.
  • Material range: Confirm compatibility with your recurring material grades and thickness range.
  • Floor space/layout: Validate infeed/outfeed flow, handling paths, and service access before finalizing the machine plan.
  • Power/voltage/export: Confirm local electrical standards, plant utilities, and export documentation/commissioning expectations.

FAQs

Quick answers to common category-level buying questions.

What production level can elbow and fittings machines support?

Output depends on elbow size mix, welding steps, operator organization, and workflow balancing with upstream duct production.

Why use dedicated fittings machines instead of manual methods?

Dedicated machines generally improve repeatability, reduce variability, and support cleaner integration with larger duct production workflows.

How do we select between different gorelocker models?

Compare your target fitting sizes, production rhythm, and layout constraints, then match the model to your most common job profile.

What material thickness considerations are important?

Start from your typical project requirements and confirm forming and welding suitability for that thickness range to avoid instability and rework.

How are installation and commissioning handled?

Typical commissioning includes machine setup, trial runs, process tuning, and operator training to stabilize fitting quality and output.

What maintenance is required for fittings equipment?

Plan routine checks for forming components, welding systems, alignment, consumables, and safety systems to keep output reliable.

Can fittings machines be combined with spiral and rectangular lines?

Yes. Many factories run dedicated fittings cells connected to both round and rectangular duct workflows.
