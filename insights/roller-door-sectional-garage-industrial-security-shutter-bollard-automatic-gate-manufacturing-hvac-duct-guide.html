1. Why roller door, sectional garage, industrial door, security shutter, bollard and automatic gate manufacturing HVAC is its own engineering discipline

An Australian roller door, sectional garage door, industrial door, security roller shutter, bollard, automatic gate and steel shed plant is one of the most diverse heavy-fabrication HVAC environments in the country. Inside a single integrated fabricator the engineering team must design for combustible aluminium fines under NFPA 484 (from extruded shutter slat, gate frame and turnstile machining), combustible steel fines under NFPA 660 (from continuous roll forming, cut-to-length, grinding and structural fabrication), chromium VI fume from stainless 316L welding at K-Rated bollard sleeves and stainless industrial door frames, manganese fume from carbon steel welding, iron oxide fume from grinding and cutting, isocyanate from polyurethane PU adhesive at the sectional panel laminator press and PU powder coat clear, zinc fume and zinc oxide ZnO particulate from the hot-dip galvanise kettle at 450 to 460 degC under AS/NZS 4680, HCl and H2SO4 acid mist from the upstream pickle line, formaldehyde from phenol formaldehyde PF resin in the powder coat polyester chemistry, VOC from MEK, IPA, acetone, ethyl acetate, toluene, xylene, MIBK at the paint booth and silicone Sikaflex polyurethane sealant bead application, and high temperature flue gas from the powder cure oven at 200 degC, the phosphate pretreatment at 60 to 80 degC, the paint cure at 60 to 80 degC, the ageing oven and the hot-dip galvanise kettle at 450 degC. Each process is manageable in isolation. Together they explain why a generic commercial HVAC fabricator treating a door, shutter, bollard or gate plant as just another industrial job replaces duct twice in the first year and walks away from the second contract.

2. The Australian regulatory stack — AS/NZS 4505, AS 4787, AS 4789, AS 4072, AS/NZS 4680, AS 1668.2, AS 4254, AS/NZS 60079, AS 1530.4, AS 3957, AS 1940, NFPA 484, NFPA 660, NFPA 86, ASTM F2656, PAS 68, IWA-14-1

An Australian door, shutter, bollard and gate manufacturer operates inside a regulatory envelope that combines product standards, building standards, dust hazard standards, fire standards, ventilation standards, combustion standards, hostile vehicle mitigation standards and counter-terrorism standards in a single integrated stack. The fabricator who understands the entire stack — rather than just the slice that applies to one process — designs HVAC ductwork that passes commissioning, audit and insurance underwriting without rework.

AS/NZS 4505 Garage doors and other large access doors is the master product standard for residential and light commercial roller doors and sectional garage doors. It specifies wind loading (cross-referenced to AS 4055 and AS 1170.2), operating force, deflection, lock and latch security, cycle life testing and assembly tolerance for the finished garage door unit. Every B&D Series 1, 2 and 3 product, every Steel-Line garage door, every Stratco roller door, every Centurion gate panel and every Spanline-fitted unit is tested and certified against AS/NZS 4505. The HVAC implication is indirect but critical: a garage door cannot pass AS/NZS 4505 weathering test if the powder coat finish fails accelerated weathering. The powder coat finish cannot pass accelerated weathering if the powder coat booth and powder cure oven HVAC fails to control humidity, dust contamination and Zone 22 dust hazard envelope.

AS 4787 Performance criteria for sectional doors sets the sectional door specific specification: panel deflection under wind load, hinge cycle life (typically 25,000 to 100,000 cycles for residential, 100,000 to 250,000 for industrial), spring counterbalance life, photocell beam safety reversal time, and finger-pinch protection. B&D Series 1, 2 and 3 sectional, Steel-Line sectional and Centurion sectional all certify against AS 4787.

AS 4789 Industrial doors and other commercial doors covers the industrial high-speed, rapid roll, accordion, sliding and folding door tier from Albany Door Systems, Dynaco, Hörmann Australia, Rite-Hite and DALMATIC Australia. Photocell beam safety, inverter VFD motor control, AS 1670 detection compatibility and AS 2118 sprinkler compatibility for fire-rated industrial doors are all in scope.

AS 4072 Components for the protection of openings in fire-resistant separating elements governs fire doors and fire shutters — the fire-rated counterpart to the standard door product. Fire-rated steel shutter is fabricated from heavy gauge 1.6 to 2.0 mm steel slat, hot-dip galvanised, with fire-resistant intumescent seal, smoke seal, fire detection link and gravity drop closer or motorised fire mode closer. The HVAC implication: AS 4072 fire doors are tested under AS 1530.4 fire resistance test with extract duct integrity at 250 degC for 2 hours minimum.

AS 1670 Fire detection, warning, control and intercom systems and AS 2118 Sprinkler systems cross-reference the fire-rated door and shutter product, governing the detection link, sprinkler head proximity and fire mode of operation.

AS/NZS 1428.1 Design for access and mobility sets the DDA (Disability Discrimination Act) compliance for door, sliding door and accessible gate product. Door opening force, threshold height, clear width and operator location all govern.

AS 1735 Lifts, escalators and moving walks intersects with the lift door product and the access gate at lift lobby.

AS 4587 Anti-vandal doors applies to school, retail and public asset shutter product.

AS 2820 Motor driven garage doors sets the electrical and motor safety standard for B&D, Steel-Line, Stratco, Centurion and the broader motorised door product.

AS 1170.2 Wind actions, AS 1170.4 Earthquake actions and AS 4055 Wind loads for housing govern the structural wind and earthquake loading on the door, shutter, bollard and gate product, plus the steel shed and portal frame downstream. Cyclone-region Far North QLD, NT and northern WA require N6, C2, C3 and C4 wind classification with enhanced fixing detail.

AS/NZS 5139 Electrical installations — Safety of battery systems for use with power conversion equipment governs the Li-ion BESS that backs up the automatic gate motor and security shutter operation. Lithium-ion battery hazard area classification under AS/NZS 60079 Zone 2 is in scope.

AS 2839 Solid plastic interior fittings and AS 2880 Door sliding manually operated cover residual product detail.

AS/NZS 4680 Hot-dip galvanised (zinc) coatings on fabricated ferrous articles is the master corrosion protection standard. Steel roller door slat, industrial door frame, bollard core, K-Rated HVM post, palisade fence picket, steel shed portal frame and farm gate all require minimum Z275 zinc coating mass. Industrial Galvanizers (Boral), Korvest, Galform, Otway and Pacific Galvanizers run the contract galvanise capacity.

AS/NZS 1554.1 Welding of steel structures, AS/NZS 1554.6 Welding stainless steels for structural purposes and AS 1665 Welding of aluminium structures govern any welded duct, bracket, transition, portal frame, bollard sleeve and gate frame fabricated locally.

AS 1668.2 The use of ventilation and airconditioning in buildings — mechanical ventilation in buildings drives the building-side HVAC envelope for the fabrication shop. Process LEV branches connect to the building extract main; building supply makeup air balances both the process and the comfort load. For a roller door, sectional door, industrial door, security shutter, bollard or gate plant the typical air change rate is 4 to 6 ACH for general fabrication, 8 to 12 ACH for the powder coat room and 6 to 10 ACH for the structural welding cell.

AS 4254 Ductwork for air-handling systems in buildings sets the duct construction grade. Parts 1 (flexible duct) and 2 (rigid duct) define gauge selection by pressure class and span, lock seam type, flange type, hanger spacing and pressure test method. For door, shutter, bollard and gate plant duct, the dominant construction grades are AS 4254 medium pressure (1000 Pa) for process branches and AS 4254 high pressure (2500 Pa) for cyclone, filter bank and hot-dip galvanise kettle inlets.

AS 1530.4 Methods for fire tests on building materials, components and structures governs fire-rated extract duct. For powder cure oven exhaust at 200 degC service temperature with potential isocyanate vapour, fire-rated extract duct is verified to 250 degC for 2 hours per AS 1530.4. Heavy gauge 1.6 to 2.0 mm carbon steel or stainless duct with continuous TIG welded longitudinal seam meets the test requirement.

AS/NZS 60079 Explosive atmospheres is the family of hazardous area classification standards. AS/NZS 60079.10.1 covers gas, vapour and mist; AS/NZS 60079.10.2 covers dust. For a door, shutter, bollard and gate plant the Zone 1 vapour areas are inside the chromate conversion bath, the powder coat spray booth and the silicone Sikaflex bead application station. The Zone 1 LPG forklift area, the propane heater feed and the adhesive solvent zone classify under AS/NZS 60079. The Zone 2 Li-ion motor BESS area applies to the automatic gate motor assembly. The Zone 22 dust hazard areas are at the extruded slat machining, structural steel cut and grind, sectional panel laminator press, powder coat booth filter house, cyclone discharge and HVM bollard concrete fill station.

AS 3957 Ventilation of fire and smoke isolated stair shafts cross-references the dust hazard area classification with explicit guidance on aluminium fines, steel fines and powder coat dust.

AS 1940 Storage and handling of flammable and combustible liquids governs the bulk storage of solvent (MEK, IPA, acetone, ethyl acetate, toluene, xylene, MIBK), thinner, adhesive, silicone Sikaflex polyurethane sealant and pre-paint cleaner.

NFPA 484 Standard for combustible metals is the global authority on aluminium, magnesium and titanium fines hazard at the extruded slat machining, gate frame end-milling, turnstile assembly grinder, punch and drill operations. The 2025 revision of NFPA 660 consolidates NFPA 484, NFPA 654 and NFPA 664 into a unified combustible dust standard.

NFPA 660 (2025) Standard for combustible dusts and particulate solids covers steel fines from continuous roll forming, cut-to-length, grinding and structural fabrication, plus powder coat dust, PU foam dust at the sectional panel insulated panel assembly and concrete dust at HVM bollard fill station.

NFPA 86 Standard for ovens and furnaces governs the powder cure oven at 200 degC, the phosphate pretreatment at 60 to 80 degC, the paint cure at 60 to 80 degC, the ageing oven, and the hot-dip galvanise kettle at 450 to 460 degC. NFPA 86 specifies burner management, flame safeguard, ventilation purge cycle, LEL monitoring on the combustion air, and explosion venting on the furnace chamber.

ASTM F2656 Standard test method for crash testing of vehicle security barriers, PAS 68 Specification for vehicle security barriers and IWA-14-1 Vehicle security barriers govern the K-Rated K4, K8 and K12 hostile vehicle mitigation HVM bollard product at Saferoads Yatala QLD plus Apollo Bollard plus Yates Steel plus Pacific Bollards plus Highway Traffic Products. K4 stops a 6.8 tonne vehicle at 50 km/h, K8 stops the same at 65 km/h, K12 stops the same at 80 km/h. The ratings drive structural steel grade, concrete fill specification, foundation engineering and stainless 316L marine grade outer sleeve specification.

AS/NZS 1715 and AS/NZS 1716 Respiratory protective equipment set the last-line-of-defence respiratory protection programme. P2 and P3 mechanical filter for aluminium, steel, powder coat dust. Acid gas cartridge for HCl pickle, H2SO4 pickle, HF aluminium pre-paint etch service. Combination cartridge for isocyanate at powder cure oven exhaust maintenance and Sikaflex polyurethane bead application.

NCC Volume 1 and 2 (National Construction Code) sets the building code envelope across the regulatory framework, including state variations under VBA Victoria, NSW Fair Trading, QLD QBCC, WA Building Commission, SA Consumer and Business Services CBS, TAS, ACT Building Authority and NT Building Commission.

The state regulatory framework adds NSW Fair Trading, VIC Victorian Building Authority VBA, QLD Queensland Building and Construction Commission QBCC, WA Building Commission, SA Consumer and Business Services CBS, TAS Consumer Building and Occupational Services CBOS, ACT Access Canberra Building Authority and NT Building Commission. SafeWork Australia plus the state WHS regulators (SafeWork NSW, WorkSafe Victoria, Workplace Health and Safety Queensland WHSQ, WorkSafe WA, SafeWork SA, WorkSafe Tasmania, WorkSafe ACT and NT WorkSafe) govern the workplace exposure and engineering control framework.

3. Steel coil decoiler, uncoiler, leveller, roll former and cut-to-length — Lysaght BlueScope Truecore and Colorbond, B&D Doors, Stratco, Steel-Line, Spanline, Bradbury Samco Tishken JMP

Continuous roll forming is the upstream process that turns Lysaght BlueScope steel coil into roof, wall, structural section, truss, sliding door panel, roller door slat (75 to 100 mm steel) and security shutter slat (50 to 80 mm steel). The process runs in five stages: steel coil decoiler and uncoiler; coil leveller to remove residual coil set; lubricator (Quaker Houghton and Castrol roll former oil); continuous roll former (typically 12 to 24 stations forming the slat profile through progressive bending); cut-to-length flying shear at the line exit; and stacker or coiler. The dominant equipment brands are Bradbury Group, Samco, Tishken and JMP supplied directly from the manufacturer to the captive and contract roll former sites across Australia.

Lysaght (the BlueScope ASX:BSL subsidiary) supplies the upstream steel coil with Truecore (zinc-magnesium-aluminium ZINCALUME plus magnesium for enhanced corrosion resistance) and Colorbond (pre-painted Zincalume) feeding the roller door slat, steel cladding and structural section market. B&D Doors, Stratco, Steel-Line, Spanline Patio Roofing, Wide Bay Steel, Hi-Light Australia, Spanlift and the broader steel shed and door fabricator base draw on the Lysaght coil supply.

The HVAC envelope at a roll former line is modest compared to downstream chemistry processes but still significant. The roll former oil mist generated by the lubricator-applied roll former oil at the forming station is captured by a low temperature LEV with mist eliminator. The oil mist STEL is 5 mg/m3 (mineral oil mist). Capture is dedicated 0.9 to 1.0 mm galvanised LEV at 10 to 14 m/s capture velocity over the roll former line, ducted to a coalescing mist filter with oil recovery. The recovered oil is recycled back into the lubricator feed.

The cut-to-length flying shear zone generates a low to moderate steel fines load, ducted at 18 to 22 m/s transport velocity to a bag filter or cartridge filter. Iron oxide Fe2O3 STEL is 5 mg/m3. The fines load is much lower than at a structural steel cut-and-grind operation because the shear cut is clean and the slat is roll-formed (not machined).

The stacker or coiler zone generates no significant fume or dust.

The SBKJ machine fitment for the roll former line zones is the SBAL-V auto duct line at galvanised setting for 0.9 to 1.0 mm low temperature LEV mains, the SBFB-1500 spiral former for round duct on the cut-to-length flying shear extract, the SBLR-600 lock former for rectangular branch duct at the mist eliminator hood, and the SBPC1500 plasma cutter for custom hood geometry at the roll former line transitions.

4. Roller shutter slat continuous roll forming — Bradnam, Boss Roller Shutters, Aalux, Stratco, Magrini, Pacific Roller Shutters, Westral, ARSG, AS 4505, AS 4787, AS 4789

Roller shutter slat continuous roll forming is the parallel upstream process to roof and wall cladding roll forming, but with a tighter slat geometry and a stronger split between steel and aluminium product. The product is continuous roll formed steel slat at 75 to 100 mm width (typically 0.5 to 0.8 mm Lysaght BlueScope Colorbond pre-painted Zincalume) for roller doors, industrial doors and large commercial shutters; or extruded aluminium slat at 50 to 80 mm width (typically 1.0 to 1.5 mm wall thickness) for residential security shutters, light commercial shutters and architectural shutters where weight is the constraint.

Bradnam, Boss Roller Shutters, Aalux, Stratco (the diversified player with extruded aluminium slat in their security shutter range), Outback Roller Shutters, Magrini, Pacific Roller Shutters, Westral and Australian Roller Shutters Group ARSG run the captive fabrication operations. The continuous roll formed steel slat line feeds from Lysaght BlueScope Colorbond coil through a Bradbury-style roll former; the extruded aluminium slat is sourced from Capral, Sapa, Alspec or imported and machined locally for slat link, end cap, guide rail, bottom rail, top axle and motor bracket assembly. Tubular electric motors from Somfy, Becker and Acmeda drive the operation under AS 2820.

The HVAC envelope at a roller shutter slat plant has three streams.

The continuous roll former for steel slat mirrors the section 3 roll former line above — oil mist at the lubricator station, low steel fines at the cut-to-length shear, captured by 0.9 to 1.0 mm galvanised LEV at 10 to 14 m/s for oil mist and 18 to 22 m/s for steel fines.

The aluminium slat machining centre for extruded aluminium slat punching, drilling, end-milling and routing generates aluminium chip and aluminium oxide fines under NFPA 484. Aluminium STEL is 1 mg/m3 TWA, aluminium oxide 10 mg/m3 inhalable and 5 mg/m3 respirable. Aluminium fines are NFPA 484 combustible metal with Al + H2O water reactivity. Capture is dedicated 0.9 to 1.2 mm galvanised spiral at 18 to 22 m/s transport velocity from the SBFB-1500 to an explosion-vented or explosion-suppressed cartridge filter bank with downstream HEPA polish, conductive bonded throughout, earth-bonded to building structure, no spark sources within 6 m of duct, no aluminium-to-aluminium friction at any duct fitting, and inerted collection with mineral oil before bagging. AS 3957 plus AS/NZS 60079.10.2 plus NFPA 484 plus NFPA 660 (2025) define the dust hazard area.

The slat link and end cap assembly station at ambient temperature generates low load fume from any solvent-based adhesive used in the link bonding operation. VOC STEL applies: MEK 200 ppm, IPA 400 ppm, acetone 250 ppm, ethyl acetate 200 ppm. Capture is low load LEV with carbon filter polish.

The SBKJ machine fitment for the roller shutter slat zone is the SBFB-1500 spiral former for 1.0 to 1.2 mm galvanised spiral aluminium fines extract duct, the SBTF-1500 / SBTF-1602 / SBTF-2020 spiral former for larger trunk mains to the cartridge filter bank, the SBAL-III auto duct line for rectangular hood plenums, the SBLR-600 lock former for Pittsburgh lock seam on the hood plenum sides, and the SBPC1500 plasma cutter for custom geometry at the aluminium slat machining hood and the cartridge filter bank inlet transitions.

5. Sectional door panel assembly — B&D Series 1, 2, 3, Steel-Line, Centurion, Stratco, Spanline; insulated panel PU foam core, Bondor, Kingspan, Metecno, Sahbox, Crane Composites; continuous laminator press, adhesive, isocyanate

Sectional door panel assembly is the most chemistry-intensive process in the Australian door manufacturing sector. The product is an insulated panel sandwich with steel skin (galvanised, Colorbond, or Lysaght BlueScope) bonded to a polyurethane PU foam core (40 to 80 mm thickness depending on R-value target) via continuous laminator press at 60 to 80 degC press cure under polyurethane adhesive. The continuous laminator delivers a 1200 mm by 6000 mm panel sheet at typical cycle time 8 to 12 minutes per cycle. Downstream the panel is cut to length, hinged, sealed with weather strip, fitted with rollers and lift handles, and assembled into the sectional door product.

B&D Series 1, 2 and 3 (Australia’s biggest residential garage door range) sectional product, Steel-Line sectional, Centurion sectional and Stratco sectional all run insulated panel sandwich assembly. Spanline runs the equivalent process for patio sectional and verandah sectional products. Bondor, Kingspan, Metecno, Sahbox and Crane Composites supply the insulated panel base material to the broader market and to the captive operations.

The HVAC envelope at the sectional panel assembly has six distinct streams.

The continuous laminator press hood at 60 to 80 degC press cure under polyurethane adhesive is the dominant isocyanate exposure zone. The process runs through:
