3D Printing, Additive Manufacturing, SLA, SLS, FDM, Metal Powder Bed Fusion, DMLS, EBM & Aerospace AM HVAC Duct Guide

An Australian-positioned engineering reference for HVAC ductwork inside additive-manufacturing facilities producing aerospace, defence, medical, automotive and construction components by metal powder bed fusion (SLM, DMLS, EBM), wire arc additive manufacturing (WAAM), cold spray AM, gas atomisation, polymer powder bed fusion (SLS), stereolithography (SLA, DLP, Carbon DLS), fused deposition modelling (FDM/FFF), hot isostatic pressing (HIP), heat treatment, post-process machining and chemical etch. Written for fabricators serving Amaero International ASX:3DA (Notting Hill VIC, Australia’s biggest aerospace AM, titanium for Hypersonix Launch Systems and Defence), AML3D ASX:AL3 (Adelaide Stem Cell Park, Australian Made wire arc additive for HMAS Stirling submarine fleet, Anduril and Defence), Titomic ASX:TTT (Notting Hill VIC, cold spray AM, Boeing and Lockheed Martin partnership), SPEE3D (Darwin NT, cold spray AM, Hypersonix and DSTG), Aurora Labs ASX:A3D (Mt Marshall WA, large-format metal printer), 3D Industries Australia (Sydney and Melbourne service bureau), Stryker Australia 3D Implant, Medtronic Australia 3D, Cochlear 3D, Materialise Australia (Belgian-owned medical AM, Sydney and Melbourne, dental implant, craniofacial and orthopaedic), Anatomic Implants, Wellington Hand Surgery Centre, CSIRO Lab22 Clayton VIC (Australia’s biggest research AM facility), Royal Adelaide, Royal Melbourne, University of Sydney, Monash University ARC Manufacturing, RMIT Centre for Advanced Materials and Performance, Australian National University ANU, University of Wollongong UoW, University of Adelaide, Swinburne University, Luyten 3D (Sydney, Melbourne and SA, Australia’s first concrete 3D printer), Bastion Cycles (titanium 3D-printed bicycle), Carbon Australia and the broader Australian additive manufacturing sector.
