France HVAC Duct Fabrication — RT 2020 / RE 2020 Compliance Playbook 2026

A practical playbook for the French HVAC duct fabrication market in 2026. Covers NF DTU 68 (Documents Techniques Unifiés) framework, RE 2020 environmental regulation replacing RT 2012, HQE certification and BREEAM Très Bon, the Île-de-France data centre cluster, Grand Paris Express infrastructure, the industrial reshoring of battery gigafactories (Verkor, ACC, ProLogium) and EV manufacturing (Stellantis, Renault), location selection across Paris / Lyon / Marseille / Toulouse / Hauts-de-France, equipment capex with French BPI subsidies, charges sociales and labour cost favouring automation.

By Taokron Engineering Team · Published May 1, 2026 · 10 min read · Reviewed by Taokron QA & Engineering

Why France in 2026

France is the second-largest EU HVAC duct market after Germany. The 2026 demand profile is driven by Île-de-France data centre expansion (Paris is one of Europe's three largest data centre markets), Grand Paris infrastructure programme, industrial reshoring of battery and EV manufacturing under France 2030, RE 2020-driven existing building modernisation, and continuing residential and commercial construction. The HVAC duct market is dominated by established French fabricators (members of SYNAFEL, the French sheet metal trade association) with deep DTU 68 specification capability.

Standards and codes

  • NF DTU 68.1 — installations de ventilation mécanique (mechanical ventilation)
  • NF DTU 68.2 — exécution des installations de chauffage central (central heating)
  • NF DTU 68.3 — installations de ventilation mécanique pour bâtiments résidentiels
  • EN 1505/1506 — adopted European harmonised standards for rectangular and circular ductwork
  • EN 1507 — leakage testing for rectangular ductwork
  • RE 2020 — Réglementation Environnementale 2020, drives energy efficiency and lifecycle carbon
  • NF EN 13779 — ventilation for non-residential buildings
  • HQE certification — Haute Qualité Environnementale, French green building rating
  • BREEAM Très Bon — UK-origin green building rating widely used on French commercial projects
  • CSTB Avis Technique — French technical agreement system for products

2026 demand drivers

  • Île-de-France data centre: Paris is one of FLAP-D markets (Frankfurt, London, Amsterdam, Paris, Dublin) — major operators include Equinix (multiple sites), Digital Realty, Data4, Telehouse, Interxion. Plus emerging Marseille submarine cable hub for Mediterranean connectivity.
  • Grand Paris Express: 200 km of new metro extending Paris RER and Métro networks, 68 new stations. HVAC for stations and depots.
  • Olympics 2024 legacy: Paris 2024 Olympic Village conversion, Stade de France precinct.
  • France 2030 reshoring: battery gigafactories (Verkor Dunkirk, ProLogium Dunkirk, ACC Douvrin), EV manufacturing (Stellantis EV transition, Renault), semiconductor (STMicroelectronics expansion).
  • Aerospace: Airbus Toulouse production expansion, Safran, Thales.
  • Pharmaceutical: Sanofi (Marcy l'Étoile, Vitry), Servier, biotech expansion.
  • Hospital programme: SEGUR de la Santé hospital modernisation programme.
  • Residential modernisation: MaPrimeRénov' programme drives energy retrofit at scale.
  • Renewable energy: ongoing solar PV park and wind farm facility HVAC.

RE 2020 — what changed from RT 2012

RE 2020 replaces RT 2012 with three new headline metrics:

  • Bbio (besoin bioclimatique): bioclimatic need limits
  • Cep (consommation en énergie primaire): primary energy consumption limit
  • Ic (impact carbone): lifecycle carbon impact, including embodied carbon — new in RE 2020

For HVAC duct, RE 2020 drives:

  • Heat pump deployment (replacing gas boilers in new builds)
  • Balanced ventilation (VMC double flux) becoming standard, replacing simple extract
  • Tighter duct leakage (typically EN 1507 Class C or tighter)
  • Insulation requirements driven by energy class
  • Embodied carbon tracking on duct material (favouring locally-sourced steel)

French labour cost reality

French sheet metal labour fully-loaded annual cost (2026, EUR):

  • Senior fitter / chef d'équipe: EUR 48,000-72,000 (USD 52K-78K)
  • HVAC fitter (compagnon, CAP/Bac Pro qualified): EUR 35,000-52,000
  • Auto duct line operator: EUR 32,000-50,000
  • CWQ welder (NF EN 9606): EUR 40,000-62,000
  • QA/QC engineer (Bac+5 ingénieur): EUR 55,000-85,000
  • Chargé d'affaires (project manager): EUR 70,000-115,000

Charges sociales add approximately 75-85% on top of base wage and are included in fully loaded figure. France 35-hour work week and statutory leave reduce annual productive hours compared to other markets — fabrication shops typically size for 1,650-1,750 productive hours per worker.

Location selection

  • Île-de-France (Paris and surrounds): largest market, data centre cluster (Saint-Denis, Aubervilliers, Massy, Évry, Villebon, La Courneuve), Grand Paris infrastructure. Highest land cost in France. Industrial land lease EUR 80-180/m²/year.
  • Lyon / Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes: industrial belt, automotive, biotech (Sanofi Marcy l'Étoile). Land lease EUR 50-110/m²/year.
  • Marseille / Aix / PACA: Mediterranean port logistics, growing data centre cluster, residential. Land lease EUR 50-100/m²/year.
  • Toulouse / Occitanie: aerospace cluster (Airbus). Land lease EUR 40-80/m²/year.
  • Hauts-de-France (Lille, Dunkirk): battery gigafactory cluster (Verkor, ProLogium, ACC), industrial reshoring. Government incentives. Land lease EUR 30-65/m²/year.
  • Strasbourg / Grand Est: cross-border German market access.

French government investment subsidies

France 2030 plan and Plan de Relance offer substantial subsidies for industrial reshoring and capital equipment:

  • BPI (Bpifrance) investment loans at favourable rates
  • Crédit d'Impôt Recherche (R&D tax credit) for innovative process
  • Industrial reshoring grants for new manufacturing in Hauts-de-France and other priority regions
  • Sectoral incentives for battery, EV, semiconductor and hydrogen sectors
  • EU structural funds in qualifying regions

For HVAC duct fabricators serving France 2030 priority sectors, subsidies can offset 10-25% of capital equipment cost. Engage Bpifrance and a local ingénierie financière advisor at company formation.

FAQ

What HVAC duct standard applies in France?

NF DTU 68 (parts 1, 2, 3) plus EN 1505/1506 European harmonised standards. RE 2020 drives energy efficiency. HQE/BREEAM certification often layers additional sustainability.

What is RE 2020?

French environmental regulation replacing RT 2012, in force from 2022. Adds lifecycle carbon impact (Ic) metric on top of bioclimatic need (Bbio) and primary energy (Cep). Drives heat pump deployment, balanced ventilation, tighter duct leakage.

What sectors drive demand?

Île-de-France data centre, Grand Paris Express infrastructure, France 2030 industrial reshoring (battery gigafactories, EV, semiconductor), aerospace Toulouse, pharma, SEGUR de la Santé hospital programme, MaPrimeRénov' residential retrofit.

What is typical labour cost?

Senior fitter EUR 48-72K, fitter EUR 35-52K, line operator EUR 32-50K, welder EUR 40-62K, QA engineer EUR 55-85K, chargé d'affaires EUR 70-115K. Charges sociales 75-85% on top of base.

What is the capex?

USD 700K-1.05M (EUR 640K-960K) turnkey including freight, EU customs (0% duty), building fit-out. France 2030 / BPI subsidies can offset 10-25% on qualifying investments.
