Duct Coil Line Comparison — Iowa Precision, Engel, Vicon, Firmac and Taokron (2026)

A coil line is the largest single purchase most duct shops make, and the brochures do not compare easily: US makers quote inches and gauge, European and Asian makers quote millimetres, and every maker counts stations differently. This page lines up the published figures for the coil lines buyers actually shortlist, notes who owns which brand, and lists what to check before you sign. It is written by people who build one family of these lines, so read the Taokron rows as a vendor’s entry and check them against the others.

By Taokron Engineering Team · Published September 4, 2026 · 9 min read · Maintained by Taokron Engineering Team

Who makes what, and who owns whom

Iowa Precision and Engel are both Mestek Machinery brands, built at one site in Cedar Rapids, Iowa: Iowa Precision is positioned as the premium line and Engel as the value line, and both sell through Mestek’s US and Canadian dealers. Vicon (Pevely, Missouri) and Firmac (Scarborough, UK) both belong to the Swedish Lindab group since 2024 and 2023. Taokron is independent. So a shortlist of “Iowa Precision, Engel and Vicon” is two owners, not three. Our brand ownership map covers the rest of the industry.

Reading the table honestly

  • Feed speed is not output. Taokron publishes 18 m/min feed and Iowa Precision 75 ft/min for the Infinity; neither is ducts per shift. A line spends most of its cycle notching, shearing, flanging and wrapping. Ask every vendor for a timed cycle on a 600×400 mm (24×16 in) duct at your gauge, and for a factory acceptance test that repeats it.
  • Gauge ceilings differ by a full class. Vicon publishes 16 ga (1.5 mm), Iowa Precision 16 ga standard with a 14 ga (2.0 mm) option, Firmac 1.2 mm, Taokron SBAL-V 1.5 mm and SBAL-III/II 2.0 mm. If hospital or industrial duct at 16 ga is in your specifications, buy the line rated a class above it.
  • Coil weight sets your crane and your buying. 12,000 lb (5,400 kg) is the US norm, 20,000 lb on option; Firmac states 5,000 kg; Taokron 8,000 kg on the SBAL-III/II decoiler. Heavier coils mean fewer changeovers and a bigger crane.
  • Plasma in line or beside it. Iowa Precision’s Pro-Fabriduct integrates a full-width plasma bed; the others pair the line with a separate table (Vicon HVAC 510/520, Lockformer Vulcan, Taokron SBPC). Both work; the in-line bed saves handling, the separate table keeps fittings cutting independent of straight-duct production.
  • Software path. Mestek lines import Autodesk, Trimble, PractiCAM and SDS; Vicon runs its own Coil Line Software; Taokron takes standard cut lists and nesting output. Test your real job file on the controller before you sign, whichever line it is.
  • Flange system. US lines offer TDC or TDF formers (dual head on the Pro-Fabriduct); Firmac uses its CGF integral-flange nomenclature; Taokron fits the SBTDF TDF former or a TDC former. Translate everything to the SMACNA profile your cleats and corners fit; see our TDF flange machine comparison.
  • Footprint. A full U-shape line such as the SBAL-V needs about 14.5 × 5 m; straight lines with a 10–14 ft transfer need 12–15 m of floor plus run-out. Measure before you shortlist.

FAQ

Which coil line is fastest?

No maker publishes ducts per shift. Iowa Precision states up to 75 ft/min line speed for the Fabriduct Infinity, Taokron 18 m/min (59 ft/min) feed for the SBAL lines, Vicon claims the fastest servo feed without a figure, and Engel states a 60-second wrap for a 16 x 16 in duct. Compare timed cycles on one duct size at your gauge, witnessed in a factory acceptance test.

Are Iowa Precision and Engel competitors?

No. Both are Mestek Machinery brands built in Cedar Rapids and sold through the same dealers; Iowa Precision is the premium line and Engel the value line. Vicon and Firmac are likewise both Lindab companies.

What coil width should I buy?

Match your coil supplier: 48 and 60 in in North America, 1250 and 1550 mm in metric markets. Firmac publishes 1000–1500 mm on the ADF and 1500 mm with a 2000 mm option on the Pro-Duct DRS; Taokron lines take 1250 or 1550 mm.

Do I need a 14 gauge (2.0 mm) line?

If 16 ga (1.5 mm) duct appears in your specifications, buy a line rated above it: Iowa Precision with the 14 ga option, or Taokron SBAL-III/II at 2.0 mm. Lines rated to 16 ga run their heaviest gauge at the edge of capacity.

Should plasma be in the line or beside it?

Either works. The Iowa Precision Pro-Fabriduct integrates a full-width plasma bed and saves handling; a separate table (Vicon HVAC 510/520, Lockformer Vulcan, Taokron SBPC) keeps fittings cutting independent of straight-duct production and is cheaper to add later.

Will duct from a different line fit my existing duct?

Yes, if seam and flange profile match. Pittsburgh and snap lock are SMACNA seams; TDF (T-25b) and TDC (T-25a) are standard flanges. Translate Firmac’s CGF nomenclature to the SMACNA profile your cleats fit and confirm with a sample joint.
