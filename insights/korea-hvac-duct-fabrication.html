Korea HVAC Duct Fabrication — 2026 Semiconductor and Battery Playbook

A practical playbook for the South Korean HVAC duct fabrication market in 2026, with focus on the two demand engines that distinguish Korea: semiconductor cleanroom HVAC (Samsung, SK hynix) and the K-Battery Belt (LG Energy Solution, Samsung SDI, SK On). Covers KS standards, ISO 14644 cleanroom HVAC for semiconductor megafabs at Pyeongtaek and Cheongju, K-Cluster and the Yongin Mega-Cluster development, location selection across Greater Seoul / Cheongju / Pohang / Songdo, equipment capex, labour cost and the Taokron equipment list for Korean fabricators.

By Taokron Engineering Team · Published May 1, 2026 · 10 min read · Reviewed by Taokron QA & Engineering

Why Korea in 2026

Korea is the world's largest memory semiconductor manufacturer (Samsung and SK hynix combined produce over 60% of global memory) and home to three of the world's largest EV battery manufacturers (LG Energy Solution, Samsung SDI, SK On). The 2026 demand profile is dominated by Samsung's Pyeongtaek P3/P4 expansion, the Yongin Mega-Cluster (300 trillion KRW investment by 2042), SK hynix Cheongju M16 and Yongin expansion, and the K-Battery Belt expansion. Cleanroom HVAC for semiconductor megafabs is the highest-margin segment and demands ISO 14644 Class 5 quality with continuous-weld stainless duct construction.

Standards and codes

  • KS B 6361 — Korean Standard for HVAC ductwork
  • KS F 2402 — testing methods for ducts
  • SMACNA — dominant on Samsung, SK hynix and major FDI cleanroom projects
  • JIS — applies on Japanese-led FDI
  • ASHRAE 90.1, 62.1 — widely referenced
  • KOSHA standards — Korea Occupational Safety and Health Agency
  • ISO 14644 — cleanroom classifications for semiconductor and pharma
  • SEMI F49 and SEMI F66 — semiconductor industry-specific HVAC clauses for sub-fab and chase work

2026 demand drivers

  • Samsung semiconductor: Pyeongtaek P3 and P4 fab expansion, Yongin Mega-Cluster (5 fabs by 2042), Hwaseong fab modernisation. World's largest planned semiconductor capacity expansion.
  • SK hynix: Cheongju M16, Yongin expansion, overseas modernisation. Major HBM (High Bandwidth Memory) capacity expansion.
  • K-Battery Belt: LG Energy Solution Cheongju and Ochang, Samsung SDI Cheonan, SK On Seosan and Cheongju. Korean battery makers also have major US (Georgia, Tennessee, Indiana) and EU footprint requiring HVAC duct supply.
  • Display: Samsung Display Tangjeong (OLED), LG Display Paju (OLED).
  • Shipbuilding: HD Hyundai Heavy Industries (Ulsan), Samsung Heavy Industries (Geoje), Hanwha Ocean (Geoje).
  • Pharmaceutical and biotech: Samsung Biologics Songdo (world's largest CMO with Plant 5), Celltrion, SK Biopharm.
  • Commercial real estate: Seoul Gangnam, Yongsan, Songdo IBD.
  • Healthcare modernisation: KOMA hospital programme.
  • Hyperscale data centre: Naver Cloud, Kakao, KT, Microsoft Korea, Equinix Korea.

Semiconductor cleanroom HVAC — what makes Korea different

Samsung and SK hynix semiconductor megafabs are the largest single HVAC duct supply contracts in the world. A new Samsung memory fab can require 50,000-100,000 m² of HVAC ductwork over 18-30 months of construction. Specifications:

  • ISO 14644 Class 5 or tighter cleanroom envelope
  • Stainless steel 316L primary, electropolished interior surface (Ra 0.4 µm)
  • Continuous TIG-welded transverse joints and longitudinal seams
  • Sub-fab ductwork per SEMI F49 with chemical resistance for solvent and acid exhausts
  • FFU (Fan Filter Unit) integration with HEPA/ULPA filters
  • Dedicated process exhaust (acid scrubber, solvent abatement, flammable scrubber)
  • Tight leakage class — typically zero allowable on critical air-handling sections

For HVAC duct fabricators, semiconductor megafab supply is a specialist segment requiring stainless coil decoiler, polished tooling, TIG welding stations and electropolishing capability. Taokron supplies the SBTF-1500 with stainless package and the SB-FS1535L stainless rectangular duct line as the standard equipment package for semiconductor cleanroom fabricators.

K-Battery Belt — the second engine

The K-Battery Belt connecting Cheongju, Ochang, Cheonan, Seosan in Chungcheongbuk-do and Chungcheongnam-do hosts the manufacturing facilities of the three Korean battery majors. Battery facility HVAC is high-margin (large industrial buildings, dust control requirements, dry room environment for cell assembly). Specifications:

  • Dry room HVAC with extremely low dew point (-40°C to -60°C) for electrolyte filling areas
  • NMP (N-Methyl-2-pyrrolidone) recovery exhaust on coating lines
  • Fire-rated construction in electrolyte storage areas
  • Cleanroom HVAC for cell assembly (typically ISO 14644 Class 7-8)
  • Tight leakage class for energy efficiency (PUE-equivalent metrics)

Location selection

  • Greater Seoul / Gyeonggi (Suwon, Anyang, Gunpo, Ansan): largest market, deep sheet metal industry, near Pyeongtaek and Yongin semiconductor megafabs. Land lease KRW 80,000-180,000/m²/year (USD 60-135/m²).
  • Cheongju (Chungcheongbuk): SK hynix and K-Battery Belt cluster. Land lease KRW 50,000-110,000/m²/year.
  • Cheonan / Asan / Seosan (Chungcheongnam): Samsung Display, K-Battery, automotive. Land lease similar to Cheongju.
  • Pohang / Ulsan (Gyeongsangbuk / Gyeongsangnam): heavy industry, shipbuilding, automotive (Hyundai Motor). Land lease KRW 40,000-90,000/m²/year.
  • Gumi / Daegu: electronics manufacturing.
  • Songdo (Incheon): Samsung Biologics, Celltrion biotech cluster. Industrial land scarce, premium.

FAQ

KS B 6361 baseline. SMACNA dominant on Samsung/SK hynix semiconductor cleanrooms and FDI. JIS on Japanese-led FDI. ISO 14644 for cleanroom classifications. SEMI F49/F66 for semiconductor sub-fab.

Samsung Pyeongtaek/Yongin semiconductor expansion, SK hynix Cheongju/Yongin, K-Battery Belt (LGES/Samsung SDI/SK On), Samsung Biologics Songdo, shipbuilding, display, hyperscale data centre.

Greater Seoul/Gyeonggi (largest, near Pyeongtaek and Yongin), Cheongju (SK hynix + battery cluster), Cheonan/Asan/Seosan (display, battery, automotive), Pohang/Ulsan (heavy industry), Songdo (biotech).
