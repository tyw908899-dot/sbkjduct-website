Why Thailand in 2026

Thailand is a mature ASEAN manufacturing hub with deep automotive industry (the "Detroit of South-East Asia"), strong electronics manufacturing and a fast-growing data centre cluster. The Eastern Economic Corridor (EEC) provides tax incentives that drive concentration of new manufacturing FDI in Chonburi, Rayong and Chachoengsao. Taokron has supplied auto duct lines and spiral tubeformers to Thai fabricators serving major EEC projects since 2018.

Standards and codes

  • TIS (Thai Industrial Standards) — local baseline
  • Building Energy Code — energy efficiency requirements
  • SMACNA — dominant on international FDI, EEC, hyperscale, hospitality
  • JIS — Japanese-led FDI (Toyota, Honda, Mazda, Nissan, Mitsubishi, Isuzu)
  • EN 1505 — European-led FDI
  • ASHRAE 90.1, 62.1 — referenced on international system designs
  • TREES (Thai Rating of Energy and Environmental Sustainability) — local green building rating

2026 demand drivers

  • Automotive FDI: Toyota, Honda, Mazda, Nissan, Mitsubishi, Isuzu — Thailand is the largest automotive manufacturer in ASEAN. Continuing EV transition (BYD, Great Wall, BMW EV).
  • Electronics FDI: Sony, Western Digital, Seagate, Hana Microelectronics, Delta Electronics. EEC has emerging semiconductor packaging FDI.
  • EEC infrastructure: U-Tapao Airport expansion, EEC high-speed rail, Eastern Aviation City.
  • Hyperscale data centre: STT GDC (Bangkok, Bang Na), AIS, NTT Telcom, Equinix, ST Telemedia, Microsoft Azure planning.
  • Commercial real estate: Bangkok CBD (Sukhumvit, Sathorn, Silom), Phuket commercial.
  • Tourism megaprojects: Phuket, Koh Samui, Pattaya expansion.
  • Healthcare modernisation: Bumrungrad, Bangkok Dusit Medical Services, BNH expansion.
  • Pharmaceutical: GPO and Pharma FDI manufacturing.

EEC and BOI promotion

EEC offers significant tax and operational benefits for new manufacturing investment:

  • Corporate income tax exemption for 8-13 years depending on activity classification
  • Import duty exemption on machinery and raw materials for export production
  • Smart Visa for foreign experts
  • Land lease at attractive industrial park rates
  • Streamlined permitting via EEC Office (EECO) one-stop service

BOI (Board of Investment) approval is required to access EEC benefits. The application process takes 60-90 days and requires demonstrated value-add commitment. Most HVAC duct fabricators serving EEC FDI customers register under BOI to access duty-free machinery import.

Location selection

  • Greater Bangkok (Pathum Thani, Samut Prakan, Nonthaburi): largest market, deepest sheet metal industry. Industrial parks: Bangkadi, Bangphoo, Bang Pa-In. Land lease ~USD 30-65/m²/year.
  • Rayong / Chonburi (EEC): closest to automotive and electronics FDI. WHA, Amata City, Hemaraj industrial estates. Land lease ~USD 25-55/m²/year with BOI benefits.
  • Ayutthaya: established automotive supplier cluster, Hi-Tech industrial estate, Bangpa-In industrial estate. Land lease ~USD 25-50/m²/year.
  • Chachoengsao (EEC north zone): emerging EEC industrial corridor.

Capex with BOI exemption

Equipment ex-works Taokron is quoted on request. Add 8-15% for freight to Laem Chabang Port plus Thai customs. With BOI-promoted EEC registration: machinery import duty-free, saving USD 30,000-65,000. Add USD 35,000-90,000 for building lease and utilities. Total turnkey capex is quoted on request.

Thai labour cost

  • Senior fitter / supervisor: USD 10,000-18,000/year
  • HVAC fitter (skilled): USD 5,500-11,000/year
  • Auto duct line operator: USD 7,500-14,000/year
  • Sheet metal welder (qualified): USD 8,500-15,000/year
  • QA/QC engineer (graduated): USD 18,000-30,000/year
  • Project manager: USD 28,000-48,000/year

Thai engineering talent is strong; Chulalongkorn University, KMUTT, Mahidol produce qualified mechanical engineers. Sheet metal labour pool is deep in Greater Bangkok and Eastern Seaboard.

FAQ

What HVAC duct standard applies?

TIS baseline; SMACNA dominant on international FDI/EEC/hyperscale; JIS on Japanese FDI; EN 1505 on European FDI.

What is the EEC?

Eastern Economic Corridor — flagship industrial development zone in Chonburi/Rayong/Chachoengsao with 8-13 year corporate tax exemption, duty-free machinery import, attractive land lease.

Where should I locate?

Greater Bangkok (largest), Rayong/Chonburi EEC (FDI proximity), Ayutthaya (automotive supplier cluster), Chachoengsao (EEC north).

What is the capex?

USD 540-810K turnkey BOI-promoted EEC, saving USD 30-65K via duty-free machinery import. Without BOI USD 580-870K.

What is typical labour cost?

Senior fitter USD 10-18K, fitter USD 5.5-11K, line operator USD 7.5-14K, welder USD 8.5-15K, QA engineer USD 18-30K.
