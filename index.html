Our machines are not assembled from commodity components. Core forming mechanisms, control systems, and tooling are developed and manufactured in-house, protected by 60+ active patents. Each machine leaves our factory against a documented inspection protocol.

Our Engineering Story →

Patented Flying Slitter

Cuts spiral duct at full strip speed without stopping the line — eliminating the cycle-time penalty of indexed cutting. Proprietary mechanism on the SBTF‑1602.

Closed-Loop CNC Control

Servo-driven length measurement with real-time feedback on every auto duct line. Enter dimensions once — the machine holds ±0.5 mm across an entire production run.

Multi-Material Tooling

Galvanized steel, stainless steel and aluminum on the same machine. Engineered for the material mixes real factories run — not laboratory conditions.

ISO 9001 Process Control

Every machine passes a documented pre-shipment inspection: dimensional accuracy, electrical integrity, motion performance. Signed off before loading.
