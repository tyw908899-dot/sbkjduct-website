Duct machines, built in our own factory since 1994

SBKJ started in 1994 as a family factory in Jiangyin, China, with one spiral tubeformer. Today we build everything a duct shop needs: auto duct lines, spiral tubeformers, TDF flange machines and elbow machines. They run in more than 100 countries.

What we build

From one machine to a whole duct shop. Tell us what you make and how much a day, and we put the right machines together for you.

Rectangular duct

Auto Duct Line V (SBAL-V)

U-shape automatic duct line: coil in, duct panels out. 1 operator, 2,000-2,500 m² a day.

Auto Duct Line III (SBAL-III)

Automatic duct line with hydraulic notching and shearing. 1-2 operators, 1,200-1,800 m² a day.

TDF Flange Forming Machine

Forms the TDF flange profile, so duct sections bolt together with corners and clips.

Lockformer (Pittsburgh)

Forms the Pittsburgh lock seam on duct panels and fittings.

Duct Zipper (seam closing)

Closes the duct seam automatically, PLC controlled. Nobody hammers seams by hand.

Corner Inserting Machine

Presses TDF corners into the flange ends of each duct section.

Auto Corner Making Machine

Makes your own TDF corners, up to 120 pieces a minute.

Pneumatic Folding Machine

Folds cut fitting panels: elbows, reducers, offsets.

CNC Plasma Cutter

Cuts flat patterns for elbows, transitions, offsets and tees.

Spiral and round duct

Spiral Tubeformer SBTF-1602

Spiral round duct Ø80-1,600 mm. Patented flying slitter and automatic length control.

Spiral Tubeformer SBTF-1500

Spiral round duct Ø80-1,500 mm with saw blade cut-off. The lower-cost spiral machine.

Gorelocker SBEM-1250

Locks the gores together into a finished round elbow, Ø100-1,250 mm.

A family factory, not a trading company

SBKJ is run by the Tao family. We design and build the machines in our own factory in Zhutang, Jiangyin, about two hours from Shanghai.

You are welcome to visit the factory. If you cannot come, ask for a live video call from the workshop floor and see your machine being built.

Bought an SBKJ machine through someone else?

Not every machine sold under the SBKJ name was built in our factory. If you are not sure about yours, send us a photo of the nameplate and the serial number. We check it against our production records and tell you.
