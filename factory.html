Production Workflow

How Factory Work Is Organized

Built from real SBKJ factory references, this flow shows how workshop fabrication, assembly, testing, and export prep connect in one delivery process.

01

Manufacturing Workshop

Core machine structures and modules are produced under controlled workshop conditions for repeatable quality.

02

Machine Assembly

After fabrication, each machine is assembled against the confirmed configuration so controls, forming units, and structure are aligned before test running.

03

Testing & Inspection

Before shipment, equipment is checked for operating stability and forming performance to support smooth startup on site.

04

Shipping & Packing

Export packing and loading are prepared by shipment plan to protect machinery in transit and support smoother customs and site receiving workflow.

05

Production Capability

With decades of specialization, SBKJ supports projects from single machines to complete duct production lines.
