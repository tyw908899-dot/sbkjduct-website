Quality Control

Quality Control for Stable Machine Performance

SBKJ has manufactured HVAC duct machinery since 1995. Our quality work is organized from procurement through shipment so industrial buyers can evaluate process discipline, not just final appearance.

Inspection Stages

How Quality Is Controlled Through the Build Cycle

Each stage focuses on practical risk reduction: verify input quality, control process consistency, validate machine operation, and confirm shipment readiness.

Incoming Material & Component Inspection

Procurement and in-house production are managed under an ISO 9001 process framework. Core materials, critical components, and key dimensions are checked before release to manufacturing.

Production Process Inspection

Machining, fabrication, and assembly are controlled by defined process steps. In-house tooling and dedicated production lines help maintain repeatable tolerances across builds.

Machine Testing

Before shipment, machine operation and forming performance are tested for stability. Electrical integrity, motion behavior, and cycle function are reviewed before sign-off.

Pre-Shipment Inspection

Every shipment follows a documented pre-shipment inspection protocol. Final verification is completed before export packing and loading.

Consistency & Reliability Focus

Quality control is structured to support repeatable output over long production runs, so buyers can plan line performance with greater confidence after installation.

Quality References
