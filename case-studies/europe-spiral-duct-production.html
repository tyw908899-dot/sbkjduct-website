SBTF-1500 for Industrial Ventilation Duct Manufacturing

A Polish industrial ventilation manufacturer adding spiral duct production capacity to complement their existing rectangular duct line — machine integration, tooling for GI and stainless steel, and production range achieved in first year of operation.

Machine: SBTF-1500 Spiral Duct Φ80–Φ1000mm GI + Stainless Steel Industrial Ventilation Poland / Europe

Situation Before: Rectangular Duct Only

The manufacturer had been producing rectangular HVAC duct for industrial ventilation projects in Poland and the broader CEE region for over a decade. Their existing line — a TDF rectangular duct line producing up to 800 m²/day — was profitable and well-utilised.

The gap in their product offering was spiral duct. Industrial ventilation projects increasingly specified round spiral duct for extraction runs — especially in food processing, pharmaceutical, and manufacturing facilities where smooth internal surfaces and clean joints were required by process specifications. The buyer was subcontracting all round duct supply to a Czech supplier, losing margin and project control on jobs where they wanted to supply the full duct package.

Annual spiral duct volume being subcontracted: approximately 35,000 metres across diameters Φ100–Φ600mm, weighted heavily toward Φ200–Φ400mm range.

Machine Selection: SBTF-1500

The buyer's key requirement was the ability to produce both GI (standard ventilation duty) and stainless steel (food-grade and chemical-resistant applications) without requiring separate machines. The SBTF-1500 was selected over the SBTF-1602 on this basis — the 1500 series supports stainless steel up to 0.8mm in its standard configuration, which covered the buyer's SS material range.

CE marking was a non-negotiable requirement for the Polish buyer — their facility operates under EU machinery directive compliance and all production equipment must carry the declaration of conformity. SBKJ supplied the CE documentation package with the machine.

Integration with Existing Rectangular Duct Line

The SBTF-1500 was installed in an adjacent bay to the existing rectangular duct line. Integration was primarily logistical — shared coil storage, shared crane access for coil loading, and a shared QC staging area. The two lines operate independently with no mechanical or electrical linkage.

Staffing integration: the buyer assigned one existing operator — previously handling material prep on the rectangular line — to operate the SBTF-1500 after a one-week intensive training programme during commissioning. A second operator role was added for coil handling and output management when the line runs two shifts.

The margin improvement on spiral duct — from resale margin to direct production margin — was the dominant financial benefit. On 37,500 metres at an average margin improvement of approximately PLN 12–18/metre, the annual improvement in gross contribution was estimated at PLN 450,000–675,000 (~EUR 100,000–150,000). Machine payback on this basis: under 24 months.

Relevant Context for European Buyers

European buyers evaluating Chinese spiral duct machinery frequently raise three concerns. This installation addressed all three:

CE marking: SBKJ supplies a CE declaration of conformity for machinery directive 2006/42/EC. The documentation package covers the machine's safety systems, electrical compliance, and risk assessment. Some buyers engage a local TÜV or notified body to verify; SBKJ provides the technical file to support this process.

Stainless steel capability: The SBTF-1500 handles 304 and 316L stainless in the 0.4–0.8mm range without separate machines or tool changes (strip width and speed adjustments only). For thicker SS (above 0.8mm), discuss the specific requirement with Ryan before specifying — forming head pressure and tooling life considerations apply.

After-sales support: Remote diagnostic support is provided via video call and electronic fault logging. Spare parts (forming rollers, cutting blades, PLC components) are available from SBKJ warehouse with DHL express delivery to Poland typically 5–8 working days. The buyer confirmed one spare parts order in year one — a set of forming rollers after extended SS production — which arrived within 6 days.
