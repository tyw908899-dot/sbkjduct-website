Background: Why a Greenfield Factory

The investor group was established primarily in property development and construction contracting in East Java. Their construction arm had been procuring HVAC duct supply for large commercial building projects — shopping centres, hotels, office towers — entirely from third-party fabricators based in Jakarta and Surabaya.

The investment thesis for a greenfield duct factory was:

  • Captive supply for own projects: The group's construction arm projects required 80,000–120,000 m² of rectangular duct and 40,000+ metres of spiral duct annually. Captive production would remove third-party margin and delivery dependency.
  • Third-party sales: The Surabaya market had limited local duct fabrication capacity. A well-equipped factory could supply other contractors and HVAC installers in the region at competitive lead times.
  • Long-term asset: The factory building (a 3,500 m² industrial unit in Sidoarjo industrial zone) was owned by the group and unused. Converting it to productive manufacturing use was the preferred option over leasing it out.

Factory Layout Planning

SBKJ provided a floor layout drawing as part of the machine quotation process. The 3,500 m² building was divided into functional zones:

  • Raw material storage: 400 m² — GI coil storage on A-frame stands; strip coil storage for spiral line; overhead crane access
  • Rectangular duct production: 600 m² — SBAL-V line (28m run) with coil area, forming stations, and output staging
  • Spiral duct production: 300 m² — SBTF-1602 line with coil unwind and output racking by diameter
  • Fitting fabrication: 400 m² — plasma table, hydraulic folder, elbow machine, assembly benches
  • Finished goods staging: 800 m² — project lot staging area for daily dispatches
  • Office, utilities, WC: 200 m²
  • Future expansion reserve: 800 m² (currently empty, planned for second SBAL-V if volume warrants)

Installation Sequence

All Phase 1 machines arrived in a single 40HQ container from Nanjing. The Phase 1 installation was completed over 14 days with two SBKJ engineers on-site.

Days 1–4: Machine unloading, positioning per layout drawing, levelling on concrete pads. The SBAL-V required levelling shims across the forming section — floor variations of 8mm were found on the 28m run; all shimmed to within 1mm before first run.

Days 5–7: Electrical connection (380V/50Hz three-phase throughout facility), compressed air feed, PLC first-power-on sequence for each machine independently.

Days 8–10: SBAL-V commissioning run on 0.8mm GI — 400×200mm duct, then 800×400mm duct, then 1,000×600mm duct. All three sizes completed to ±0.8mm dimensional tolerance before moving on. SBTF-1602 commissioning at Φ200, Φ400, and Φ600mm simultaneously by second engineer.

Days 11–13: Operator training — 4 production operators trained across SBAL-V and SBTF-1602. Shift leader trained on parameter entry, fault codes, and first-level maintenance procedures.

Day 14: Full production shift — no engineer intervention. Factory produced 820 m² rectangular duct and 280 metres spiral duct in the first unsupported shift.
