What to expect after you submit

Founded in 1995, Taokron has shipped HVAC ductwork machinery to 100+ countries, with 5,000+ installations completed. Every quotation request is read by a Taokron engineer — never a sales script — and our standard response time is under 12 hours, including weekends. The reply you receive will include a recommended machine configuration, an indicative workshop layout drawing, ballpark pricing in USD, and the lead time from order confirmation to FOB Melbourne dispatch.

Information that helps us reply faster

The more specific your inquiry, the faster the engineering response. Useful inputs include:

  • Duct type and size range — round, rectangular, oval; minimum and maximum diameter or width.
  • Material and gauge — galvanized steel (G60/G90/G115), stainless 304/316, or aluminium, plus thickness range in mm or gauge.
  • Daily output target — m² of finished duct per day or shifts per week.
  • Site conditions — country, voltage and frequency (380V/415V, 50Hz/60Hz), available floor space and ceiling height.
  • Standards required — SMACNA, EN 1505/1506, AS/NZS 4254, SASO, or other regional construction standards.

Quotation process and lead times

  1. Step 1 — Initial reply (within 12 hours). A Taokron engineer reviews your inquiry and either confirms a standard configuration or asks the 2–3 follow-up questions needed to specify the right machine.
  2. Step 2 — Technical proposal (1–3 business days). Detailed specification, layout drawing, electrical and pneumatic requirements, optional accessories, total CIF or FOB pricing.
  3. Step 3 — Contract and 30% deposit. Production scheduled. Standard build time is 45–60 days for single machines, 75–120 days for full auto duct lines.
  4. Step 4 — Factory acceptance test (FAT). Optional buyer visit to Australia or live video FAT before dispatch. Final 70% balance against B/L copy.
  5. Step 5 — On-site installation and training. Taokron engineers travel to your facility for commissioning and operator training, typically 3–7 days on site per machine.

Privacy and confidentiality

The information you submit through this form is used only to prepare your quotation and is not shared with third parties. NDAs are available on request before commercial discussions, and Taokron routinely supplies machinery to OEM customers under white-label terms.

Who replies to your enquiry

Every quotation enquiry sent through this page is read first by a working Taokron mechanical engineer based at the Australian headquarters, not by a sales agent or external contractor. The first reply you receive will come from someone who has personally designed, built or commissioned the machine you are asking about, and who is also the person you will speak with during contract review, manufacturing, FAT and on‑site commissioning. Taokron does not use call centres, channel partners or freelance sales reps for international enquiries — the engineering and customer relationship is single‑threaded from first contact through warranty support.

How to write a quotation enquiry that gets a fast reply

Most enquiries Taokron receives are missing one or two pieces of information that are required to prepare a meaningful first reply. The fastest way to a useful quotation is to include all of the following in the very first message — this lets the engineer skip the back‑and‑forth and reply with a real configuration and price within hours.

  • Project type — commercial HVAC, airport, metro, data centre, cleanroom, hospital, industrial, marine, or other (see machinery by industry)
  • Daily output target — m² per day of finished duct (or shifts per week if you have a longer horizon)
  • Duct geometry — round (Ø range), rectangular (W × H range), oval, or a mix
  • Material and gauge — galvanised G60/G90, stainless 304/316, aluminium, plus thickness
  • Project standard — SMACNA, EN 1505, AS/NZS 4254, DW/144 or other
  • Site power — 380/415 V, 50/60 Hz, available main supply capacity
  • Workshop dimensions — building length × width × ceiling height, crane capacity
  • Timeline — when you need the line operational on site
  • Budget envelope — even a rough range helps us recommend the right tier

What you can expect inside a typical Taokron quotation

Taokron quotations are deliberately detailed because most international buyers compare two or three suppliers before deciding. A standard quotation runs 8–14 pages and includes: cover page with point of contact, executive summary of the recommended configuration, machine‑by‑machine specification table, layout drawing showing the line in your workshop, electrical single‑line diagram and total power demand, pneumatic and water requirements where relevant, recommended spare parts list with prices, freight estimate from Melbourne to your destination port, installation and commissioning scope, training plan, payment schedule, lead time and delivery commitment, and a separate page covering warranty and after‑sales support. You can request the quotation as PDF, Word or Excel.

Frequently asked questions

How quickly will Taokron reply to my enquiry?

The standard Taokron commitment is a first engineering reply within 12 hours of receiving your enquiry, including weekends. In practice, most enquiries are answered within 2–4 hours during Asia‑Pacific business hours. The reply will either confirm a standard configuration or list the 2–3 follow‑up questions the engineer needs to specify the right line.

Do I need to sign anything to receive a quotation?

No. The initial quotation is free and carries no commitment. Taokron can sign a mutual NDA before commercial discussions if your project has confidentiality requirements — just ask in your first message. Contracts are only signed once you have approved the technical specification and price.

Can I see the machines before deciding?

Yes. Taokron welcomes customer visits to the Australian headquarters. You can see the machines run, meet the engineering team and run sample duct on the line you are considering. Visa support letters are provided as needed. Many international buyers combine the machine demonstration with a Factory Acceptance Test on a similar line that is being built for another customer.

What if I am not ready to buy yet?

That is fine — many enquiries we receive are at the planning stage. Tell us where you are in the project and we will send a budgetary quotation, a typical layout sketch and the technical information you need to scope your CAPEX. There is no obligation, no follow-up sales pressure and no charge.

Can Taokron help with financing?

Taokron does not offer direct financing, but we can introduce qualified buyers to global export credit insurance (export credit agencies) and to international project finance brokers who have funded Taokron machinery purchases in the past. Speak to your account engineer for details.
