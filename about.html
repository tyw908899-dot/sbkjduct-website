SBKJ Group – Our Story

Milestones

Three Decades of HVAC Ductwork Machinery

From a small roll‑forming workshop in Australia to a global ductwork machinery exporter, SBKJ's history mirrors three decades of HVAC automation innovation.

  1. 1995

    Founded in Australia

    SBKJ is founded in Box Hill North, Victoria, supplying manual lockformers and plate shearing equipment for the Greater Melbourne HVAC contracting market.

  2. 2002

    First spiral tubeformer line

    SBKJ launches its first SBTF series spiral tubeformer, introducing flying‑shear cutting to sheet‑metal shops and cutting round‑duct fabrication time by more than 60 percent.

  3. 2007

    TDF flange roll‑forming

    The TDF flange roll‑forming machine enters volume production, allowing rectangular duct contractors to eliminate angle‑iron flanges and cut field labour by roughly 40 percent per square metre.

  4. 2011

    ISO 9001 & CE certification

    SBKJ certifies its full production pipeline under ISO 9001 and obtains CE marking for auto duct lines, spiral machines and plasma cutters, unlocking volume exports to the EU and Middle East.

  5. 2015

    SBAL auto duct line series

    The SBAL auto duct production line debuts with CNC plasma cutting, automatic pittsburgh seaming and TDF flanging on a single servo‑driven platform — the flagship product SBKJ still ships in SBAL‑III, SBAL‑V and RDL‑II variants today.

  6. 2018

    3,000th machine shipped

    SBKJ reaches 3,000 cumulative installations across 100+ countries, with flagship projects including metro stations in the Middle East, commercial towers in Southeast Asia and cleanroom factories in Eastern Europe.

  7. 2021

    Australia office opens

    SBKJ Group opens its Australia office in Box Hill North, Victoria, providing local sales, spare parts logistics and commissioning support for customers across Australia, New Zealand and the Pacific.

  8. 2024

    60+ patents and 5,000+ installations

    SBKJ surpasses 60 invention and utility‑model patents covering flying‑shear spiral forming, TDF roll forming, auto‑line PLC integration, servo positioning and safety interlocks. Cumulative installations exceed 5,000 machines in 100+ countries.

Certifications & standards

ISO 9001:2015, CE & 60+ Patents

Independent third‑party certification, documented factory testing and an active patent portfolio — the evidence behind every SBKJ quote.

ISO 9001:2015 quality management

SBKJ’s production pipeline is certified under ISO 9001:2015 for the design, manufacture and after‑sales service of HVAC ductwork machinery. The certification is maintained by annual surveillance audits of incoming inspection, assembly, final testing and traceability records.

CE marking (Machinery Directive)

Auto duct lines, spiral tubeformers, plasma cutters, TDF flange machines and lockformers are CE‑marked to the Machinery Directive 2006/42/EC, the EMC Directive 2014/30/EU and the Low Voltage Directive 2014/35/EU. Each shipment includes the EC Declaration of Conformity and a full technical file.

60+ invention and utility patents

SBKJ holds over 60 invention and utility‑model patents across flying‑shear spiral forming, TDF roll‑form geometry, auto‑line servo positioning, pittsburgh seaming safety mechanisms and modular tooling. New applications are filed every year through the SBKJ R&D office.

Standards supported

Every SBKJ line is configured to produce duct that complies with the international fabrication standard you specify on your order.

  • SMACNA HVAC Duct Construction Standards (USA & Middle East projects)
  • EN 1505 / EN 1506 (European rectangular and circular duct)
  • AS/NZS 4254.1 & 4254.2 (Australia and New Zealand flexible and rigid duct)
  • DW/144 (United Kingdom HVAC duct specification)

Quality process

From Sheet Metal to Factory Acceptance Test

Every SBKJ machine goes through a documented five‑stage quality process before it leaves Australia. Customers are welcome to attend the Factory Acceptance Test (FAT) in person or via video call. The full procedure is documented on the quality control page, the SBKJ production and quality process is described on the production and quality page, HD demo footage of every machine plus current machine demonstration footage is available on request through the machine video library, and the case studies library covers three real SBKJ installations in Vietnam, Saudi Arabia and Australia with before/after production numbers from the buyers themselves. If you’re comparing SBKJ against other HVAC duct machinery vendors, the why choose SBKJ page summarises the ten reasons buyers short-list SBKJ — plus the three scenarios where SBKJ is not the right choice. For transparent 2026 budget ranges, lead times, payment terms and a complete workshop budgeting framework, see the pricing and lead time buyer guide.

  1. 01

    Incoming material inspection

    Steel plate, servo motors, PLCs, bearings and hydraulic components are verified against the drawing and specification before being released to assembly. Traceability records are kept for the life of the machine.

  2. 02

    Roll tooling geometry check

    Forming rolls are measured on a CMM and checked against the SBKJ master drawing. The tolerance on the final roll diameter is within ±0.03 mm, which is what allows SBKJ spiral and TDF machines to run at production speed without seam defects.

  3. 03

    Mechanical & electrical assembly

    Assembly is performed by teams dedicated to a single machine family. Each assembler signs off the mechanical tolerances, wiring and safety interlocks. Torque values and wiring continuity are recorded on the build sheet.

  4. 04

    No‑load and loaded run‑in

    Machines run for 4–24 hours no‑load, then produce real duct on customer material. First‑article duct is measured against the project standard (SMACNA, EN 1505, AS/NZS 4254) and the result is signed by the QA engineer.

  5. 05

    Factory Acceptance Test (FAT)

    On request, SBKJ hosts the customer or their third‑party inspector for a live FAT. The machine is run through the full production cycle, safety interlocks are demonstrated and the test record is signed jointly before packing.
