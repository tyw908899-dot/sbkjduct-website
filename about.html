About SBKJ

Manufacturer of HVAC Duct Machinery for Industrial Production

SBKJ Group is an HVAC duct machinery manufacturer based in the Yangtze River Delta near Shanghai, China. Since 1995, we have focused on complete duct fabrication equipment, including auto duct lines, spiral tubeformers, round duct machines, and fittings solutions.

For industrial HVAC buyers, this means one supplier focused on throughput, machine integration, commissioning, and long-term support.

Company Overview

SBKJ has served HVAC duct producers for over three decades with equipment designed for stable output and practical workshop operation. The company is recognized as a national high-tech enterprise, with ISO9001 and CE certifications, and holds dozens of patents.

Our machinery is used in climate and ventilation projects in more than 60 countries and regions, supported by an export team experienced in international delivery.
