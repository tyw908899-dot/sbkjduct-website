About SBKJ

Manufacturer of HVAC Duct Machinery for Industrial Production

SBKJ Group is an HVAC duct machinery manufacturer based in the Yangtze River Delta near Shanghai, China. Since 1995, we have focused on complete duct fabrication equipment for industrial production.

For industrial HVAC buyers, this means one coordinated team for machine selection, integration, and long-term support.

Company Overview

SBKJ has served HVAC duct producers for over three decades with equipment designed for stable output and practical workshop operation. The company operates under ISO9001 and CE frameworks and holds dozens of patents.

Our export and engineering teams support international delivery, installation planning, and commissioning coordination for HVAC buyers across global markets.
