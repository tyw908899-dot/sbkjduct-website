002 Machine Overview

Coil in. Formed duct out.

U‑shape integrated line — coil feeding, leveling, bead rolling, hydraulic notching, shearing, Pittsburgh lock forming, flange prep, and folding in one connected workflow. 2–3 operators, 800–2,500 m²/day, ±0.5 mm CNC tolerance.

/ 01 Coil Loading & Decoiling Heavy-duty hydraulic decoiler handles coils up to 8 tons. Strip feeds into the line automatically.
/ 02 Strip Leveling & Beading Multi-roller leveling removes coil set. Bead rolling adds stiffening ribs for structural performance in duct sections.
/ 03 CNC Notching Hydraulic notching at programmed positions — corner notch geometry set via CNC for consistent fold-up joints. No manual measuring.
/ 04 High-Speed Shearing CNC-controlled shear cuts blanks to programmed length. Line feeds at 18 m/min.
/ 05 Lock & Flange Forming Pittsburgh or snap-lock profile at blank edges. TDF, angle, or drive cleat flange per order configuration.
/ 06 Folding & Panel Output Auto folding unit bends panels to rectangular duct section. Completed duct exits with minimal manual handling.

004 Typical Applications

Who this is — and isn't — for.

Best for

  • HVAC duct factories running daily high-volume batch production.
  • Shops scaling from manual forming to full-line automation.
  • Operations needing repeatable output across multiple duct sizes.
  • Workshops with TDF, angle flange, or drive cleat finishing workflows.
  • Buyers who prioritize low rework rate and dimensional consistency.

Not ideal for

  • Very small shops with under 300 m²/day requirements.
  • Operations needing primarily spiral or round duct output.
  • Environments with frequent duct size changes (under 10 different sizes/week).
  • Workshops where floor space is under ~80 m² available.
005 Before You Request a Quote

What to specify when asking for a quote.

Share these six items and Ryan returns a configured SBAL‑V proposal — typically within one business day. Skipping items means a longer back-and-forth before your real quote arrives.

/ 01

Duct size range

Smallest and largest duct section (W × H) you plan to produce. Drives the 1250U vs 1550U choice.

/ 02

Material & thickness

GI, stainless, aluminum — and the thickness band (0.5–1.5 mm). Tooling is configured at the factory to your material.

/ 03

Target daily output

m²/day target and number of shifts. Helps size coil weight, stacking, and downstream stations.

/ 04

Flange standard

TDF, angle flange, or drive cleat. Each is a tooling configuration. If you run multiple, say so — we'll discuss changeover.

/ 05

Floor space & power

Available floor (m × m), ceiling height, local voltage / frequency. We'll confirm the layout fits before shipment.

/ 06

Destination country & port

For CIF/FOB logistics and commissioning plan. Ryan coordinates export docs and on-site or remote installation.

Operator note: the more specific your inputs, the more specific the quote. Ballpark ranges are fine on first contact — we refine the proposal as your project firms up.

007 Frequently Asked

Answers from the factory floor.

What is the difference between SBAL-V-1250U and SBAL-V-1550U?

Maximum sheet width: 1,250 mm vs 1,550 mm. The 1550U handles larger duct sections and is typically chosen when your duct range includes sizes above 600 × 600 mm developed flat dimension. For most standard commercial HVAC duct ranges, the 1250U is sufficient. Discuss your full duct size range with Ryan before choosing.

How long does it take to change between duct sizes?

CNC parameter change takes 2–5 minutes for width and length adjustments. In a well-run operation, size changeovers are typically under 15 minutes with an experienced operator. Flange tooling changes (e.g. TDF to drive cleat) are longer — plan 30–60 minutes for tooling swaps.

What floor area does the SBAL-V require?

The base machine is approximately 14,500 × 4,950 × 1,500 mm. Including operator working space, coil loading area, and output stacking, a practical floor area of 25 × 12 m is a reasonable minimum for a functional layout. Floor-plan review is included in the SBKJ pre-order service.

Can the line run unattended or overnight?

The line requires operator presence for coil loading and output handling. CNC control means a trained team of 2–3 can sustain production without per-piece intervention. Unmanned overnight operation is not typical, but a single operator can supervise running production for extended periods.

What flange systems does the SBAL-V support?

TDF (transverse duct flange) roll-forming, angle flange, and drive cleat profile configurations. Standard line ships configured for one flange standard — if your operation runs multiple, discuss dual-configuration or changeover tooling at order time.

What voltage and power supply is required?

Standard is 380V / 50Hz / 3-phase, 40 kW total installed power. For 60Hz markets (Americas, Japan, Philippines, parts of Southeast Asia) or non-standard voltages, confirm your local power standard when ordering — supply can be adjusted at the factory before shipment.
