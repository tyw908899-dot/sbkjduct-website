Typical Production Workflow

01
Coil Loading & Decoiling Heavy-duty hydraulic decoiler handles coils up to 8 tons. Strip feeds into the line automatically.
02
Strip Leveling & Beading Multi-roller leveling removes coil set. Bead rolling adds stiffening ribs for structural performance in duct sections.
03
CNC Notching Hydraulic notching at programmed positions — corner notch geometry set via CNC for consistent fold-up joints. No manual measuring.
04
High-Speed Shearing CNC-controlled shear cuts blanks to programmed length. Speed maintained at 18 m/min strip feed rate.
05
Pittsburgh Lock & Flange Forming Lock forming rolls Pittsburgh or snap-lock profile. TDF flange prep or angle flange roll-forming at blank edges depending on configuration.
06
Panel Output & Folding Formed blanks exit line ready for folding. Auto folding unit bends panels to rectangular duct section — complete duct section formed with minimal manual handling.

Before and After in a Full Line

Before: Coil slitting machine to prepare strip from master coil. Pre-slit strip on individual coils also works for lower volumes.

After: Seam closing machine (SBHF-I) for duct sealing, TDF flange machine for joint finishing, elbow and fitting stations for transitions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between SBAL-V-1250U and SBAL-V-1550U?

The key difference is maximum sheet width: 1250 mm vs. 1550 mm. The 1550U handles larger duct sections and is typically chosen when your duct range includes sizes above 600mm × 600mm developed flat dimension. For most standard commercial HVAC duct ranges, the 1250U is sufficient. Discuss your full duct size range with Ryan before choosing.

How long does it take to change between duct sizes?

CNC parameter change takes 2–5 minutes for width and length adjustments. Notch geometry adjustments add time depending on degree of change. In a well-run operation, size changeovers are typically under 15 minutes with an experienced operator. Flange tooling changes (e.g., from TDF to drive cleat) are longer — plan 30–60 minutes for tooling swaps.

What floor area does the SBAL-V require?

The SBAL-V-1250U base machine is approximately 14,500 × 4,950 × 1,500 mm. Including operator working space, coil loading area, and output stacking, a practical floor area of 25m × 12m is a reasonable minimum for a functional layout. Final layout planning is part of the SBKJ pre-order service — we will review your floor plan before confirming the line configuration.

Can the line run unattended or overnight?

The line requires operator presence for coil loading and output handling. However, the CNC-controlled process means a trained operator team of 2–3 can sustain production without per-piece intervention. Unmanned overnight production is not typical, but a single operator can supervise running production for extended periods.

What flange systems does the SBAL-V support?

SBAL-V supports TDF (transverse duct flange) roll-forming, angle flange, and drive cleat profile configurations. The standard line is configured for one flange standard — if your operation runs multiple standards, discuss dual-configuration or changeover tooling requirements at the time of order.

What voltage and power supply is required?

Standard configuration is 380V / 50Hz / 3-phase, 40 kW total installed power. For 60Hz markets (Americas, Japan, Philippines, parts of Southeast Asia) or non-standard voltages, confirm your local power standard when ordering — supply configuration can be adjusted at the factory before shipment.
