Is the SBAL-V Right for Your Operation?

Built for
  • HVAC duct factories running daily high-volume batch production
  • Shops scaling from manual forming to full-line automation
  • Operations needing repeatable output across multiple duct sizes
  • Workshops with TDF, angle flange, or drive cleat finishing workflows
  • Buyers who prioritize low rework rate and dimensional consistency
Not ideal for
  • Small shops with under 300 m²/day requirements
  • Operations needing primarily spiral or round duct output
  • Environments with very frequent duct size changes
  • Workshops where floor space is under ~80 m² available

Production Workflow

Six integrated stages from coil to formed duct panel — each CNC-controlled, no manual measuring between steps.

01
Coil Loading & Decoiling Heavy-duty hydraulic decoiler handles coils up to 8 tons. Strip feeds into the line automatically at 18 m/min.
02
Strip Leveling & Beading Multi-roller leveling removes coil set. Bead rolling adds stiffening ribs for structural performance across duct sections.
03
CNC Hydraulic Notching Hydraulic notching at programmed corner positions — geometry set via CNC for consistent fold-up joints. No manual layout or measuring.
04
High-Speed CNC Shearing CNC-controlled shear cuts blanks to programmed length. Maintains 18 m/min strip feed rate with ±0.5 mm tolerance.
05
Pittsburgh Lock & Flange Forming Lock forming rolls Pittsburgh or snap-lock profile. TDF flange or angle flange roll-forming at blank edges depending on configuration.
06
Auto Folding & Panel Output Auto folding unit bends panels to rectangular duct section. Complete duct section formed with minimal manual handling between steps.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to change between duct sizes?

CNC parameter change takes 2–5 minutes for width and length. Size changeovers are typically under 15 minutes with an experienced operator. Flange tooling changes (TDF to drive cleat) take 30–60 minutes.

What floor area does the SBAL-V require?

The 1250U is approximately 14,500 × 4,950 × 1,500 mm. Including operator clearance and coil loading, a practical minimum is 25m × 12m. Final layout planning is part of the SBKJ pre-order process — we review your floor plan before confirming the line configuration.

Can the line run unattended or overnight?

The line requires operator presence for coil loading and output handling. A trained team of 2–3 can sustain production without per-piece intervention. Extended supervised runs with 1 operator are common, but full unmanned overnight production is not typical.

What flange systems does the SBAL-V support?

TDF (transverse duct flange) roll-forming, angle flange, and drive cleat profile configurations. Standard line is configured for one flange system. Discuss dual-configuration or changeover tooling requirements at time of order.

What voltage and power supply is required?

Standard: 380V / 50Hz / 3-phase, 40 kW total. For 60Hz markets or non-standard voltages, confirm your local power specification when ordering — supply configuration is adjusted at the factory before shipment.
