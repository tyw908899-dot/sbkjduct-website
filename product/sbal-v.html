Is the SBAL-V Right for You?

Best for

  • HVAC duct factories running daily high-volume batch production
  • Shops scaling from manual forming to full-line automation
  • Operations needing repeatable output across multiple duct sizes
  • Workshops with TDF, angle flange, or drive cleat finishing workflows
  • Buyers who prioritize low rework rate and dimensional consistency

Not ideal for

  • Small shops with under 300 m²/day volume requirements
  • Operations needing primarily spiral or round duct output
  • Environments with <10 duct size changes per week
  • Workshops where floor space is under ~80 m² available

For lower output targets or smaller floor space, consider the SBAL-III (I-shape layout, ~1,000 m²/shift).

Output Explained in Plain English

The 800–2,500 m²/day range reflects real-world variance by product mix, material gauge, and operating hours. Here's how to read it:

~800 m²/day — Conservative

Varied sizes, thicker gauge (1.2–1.5 mm), frequent changeovers, single shift. Typical for a new installation in its first 3 months of operation.

~1,500 m²/day — Typical

Consistent sizes, 0.7–1.0 mm galvanized steel, single shift with experienced operators. Most buyers operate in this range after 6 months.

~2,500 m²/day — Optimized

High-volume uniform sizes, thinner gauge (0.5–0.8 mm), double shift operation. Requires stable material supply and a well-run changeover process.

For a practical estimate based on your duct size range and shift schedule, discuss your mix with Ryan before finalising configuration.

Configurations: 1250U vs 1550U

The SBAL-V is available in two width configurations. The difference is maximum sheet width — which determines the largest duct section the line can produce without a material join.

SBAL-V-1250U Most common
  • Max sheet width: 1,250 mm
  • Handles duct sections up to ~600 × 600 mm developed flat
  • Suitable for most commercial HVAC duct ranges
  • Smaller machine footprint, lower base price
  • Recommended for new installations unless confirmed larger sizes needed
SBAL-V-1550U Larger output range
  • Max sheet width: 1,550 mm
  • Handles duct sections above 600 × 600 mm developed flat
  • Required for industrial or heavy-commercial duct programs
  • Larger footprint — confirm floor space before ordering
  • Choose when your duct range includes wider-section jobs

Not sure which applies to your duct size program? Share your full size range with Ryan — he'll confirm which configuration is appropriate before you commit.

Production Workflow — Step by Step

The SBAL-V runs a continuous coil-fed process. Each station feeds directly into the next — eliminating the manual handling between individual machines that causes dimensional variance in staged setups.

01
Coil Loading & Decoiling Heavy-duty hydraulic decoiler handles coils up to 8 tons. Strip feeds into the line automatically — no manual unrolling or positioning between cycles.
02
Strip Leveling & Bead Rolling Multi-roller leveling removes coil set and ensures flat strip entry. Bead rolling adds stiffening ribs for structural performance across duct section spans.
03
CNC Notching Hydraulic notching at programmed corner positions — geometry set via CNC for consistent fold-up joints. No manual measuring or layout marking required.
04
High-Speed Shearing CNC-controlled shear cuts blanks to programmed length. Line speed maintained at 18 m/min strip feed rate — cut-to-length without stopping the feed.
05
Pittsburgh Lock & Flange Forming Lock forming rolls Pittsburgh or snap-lock seam profile. TDF flange prep or angle flange roll-forming at blank edges — depending on your configured option.
06
Panel Output & Auto Folding Formed blanks exit line ready for folding. Auto folding unit bends panels to rectangular duct section with minimal manual handling — complete duct section per cycle.

Complete Line Context

Upstream: Coil slitting machine to prepare strip width from wide coil. The SBAL-V feeds from pre-slit strip coil at configured width. Some factories run a separate decoiler/feeder when operating multiple coil widths in parallel.

Downstream: The SBAL-V produces formed duct panels ready for assembly. Downstream operations typically include a seam closing machine (SBHF-I), TDF flange machine, and separate fitting fabrication stations for elbows and transitions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between SBAL-V-1250U and SBAL-V-1550U?

The key difference is maximum sheet width: 1,250 mm vs. 1,550 mm. The 1550U handles larger duct sections and is typically chosen when your duct range includes sizes above 600 × 600 mm developed flat dimension. For most standard commercial HVAC duct ranges, the 1250U is sufficient. Discuss your full duct size range with Ryan before choosing.

How long does it take to change between duct sizes?

CNC parameter change takes 2–5 minutes for width and length adjustments. Notch geometry changes add time. In a well-run operation, size changeovers are typically under 15 minutes with an experienced operator. Flange tooling swaps (e.g., TDF to drive cleat) take 30–60 minutes.

What floor area does the SBAL-V require?

The SBAL-V-1250U base machine is approximately 14,500 × 4,950 × 1,500 mm. Including operator working space, coil loading area, and output stacking, a practical floor area of 25 m × 12 m is a reasonable minimum. Final layout planning is part of SBKJ's pre-order service — we review your floor plan before confirming the configuration.

Can the line run unattended or overnight?

The line requires operator presence for coil loading and output handling. However, with a trained 2–3 operator team, production runs with minimal per-piece intervention. Unmanned overnight production is not typical, but a single operator can supervise a running production cycle for extended periods.

What flange systems does the SBAL-V support?

SBAL-V supports TDF (transverse duct flange) roll-forming, angle flange, and drive cleat profile configurations. The standard line is configured for one flange standard — if your operation runs multiple standards, discuss dual-configuration or changeover tooling requirements at time of order.

What voltage and power supply is required?

Standard configuration is 380V / 50Hz / 3-phase, 40 kW total installed power. For 60Hz markets (Americas, Japan, Philippines, parts of Southeast Asia) or non-standard voltages, confirm your local power standard when ordering — supply configuration adjusted at the factory before shipment.
