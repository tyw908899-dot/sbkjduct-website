Spiral Tubeformer SBTF‑1602

Φ80–Φ1600 mm continuous spiral duct from strip coil — patented flying shear cuts at full forming speed without stopping the line.

CE Certified ISO 9001 Patented Flying Shear 60+ Countries Installed 12-Month Warranty

Best for

  • HVAC contractors producing standard round duct diameters Φ100–Φ800mm at high daily volume
  • Factories needing continuous output without stopping to cut (flying shear maintains line speed at 35m/min through the cut cycle)
  • Operations producing stainless steel or aluminum spiral duct alongside galvanized — all three materials supported on one machine
  • Workshops replacing saw-blade cut machines to eliminate blade consumable cost and burr on the cut face

Not ideal for

  • Operations needing only Φ80–Φ200mm small-diameter duct at modest daily volume (SBTF-1500 may be more economical)
  • Factories with strip coil width below 90mm or above 300mm (outside the SBTF-1602 strip range)
  • Very low-volume production under 50m/day where the capital cost of flying shear technology is not justified by output gains

Machine Overview

The SBTF-1602 forms spiral round duct continuously from strip coil — galvanized steel, stainless steel, or aluminum — at Φ80 to Φ1600mm diameter. The strip feeds from a motorised decoiler, passes through a forming head that locks the strip into a helical seam, and the formed tube is cut by a flying shear that travels with the tube at forming speed. The line does not stop or slow during cutting.

The patented flying shear system (patent applied in Jiangyin, active) eliminates the stop-start cutting cycle of saw-blade machines. At 35m/min strip feed rate, the SBTF-1602 produces approximately 8m of Φ200mm duct per minute. Diameter changes are made by adjusting the forming head — no tooling change required for diameter steps within the Φ80–Φ1600mm range.

Output per shift (8hr) at typical production conditions: Φ200mm GI at 0.6mm — approximately 2,400 linear metres. Diameter and material gauge affect output proportionally; larger diameter runs slower due to the longer cut cycle time required at greater tube circumference.

Production Workflow

01
Coil Loading Strip coil loaded onto motorised decoiler. Standard max coil weight: 2 tons. Heavy-duty decoiler option available for 5-ton coils.
02
Strip Feeding and Leveling Strip feeds through leveling rollers to remove coil curl before entering the forming head. Consistent strip flatness is critical for seam lock quality.
03
Helical Seam Forming Strip enters multi-roller forming head and is locked into a helical lock seam as the tube forms continuously. No welding — mechanical lock seam joins strip edges as the helix tightens.
04
Flying Shear Cut CNC triggers the flying shear at the programmed tube length. The shear carriage accelerates to match tube speed, cuts, and retracts — all without stopping or slowing the strip feed. Length accuracy: ±2mm over 3m.
05
Cut Tube Discharge Cut tube exits onto run-out table (6,000×800×900mm standard). Operator stacks finished tubes or feeds directly to a flanging station downstream.
06
Diameter Change Forming head adjusted via handle and graduated scale. Close-range changes (e.g., Φ250 to Φ315mm) take 3–5 minutes. Larger steps (e.g., Φ200 to Φ800mm) take 8–12 minutes including test cut verification.

What Goes Before and After This Machine

Before the SBTF-1602: A strip slitting machine if purchasing master coil and cutting to width in-house. Typical strip widths used: 90–300mm depending on diameter. Pre-slit strip on individual coils is viable for lower volumes or operations without in-house slitting capacity. Coil handling equipment (coil car or crane) recommended for coils above 1 ton.

After the SBTF-1602: Spiral duct flanging machine for TDF or angle-iron ends; plasma cutter for fitting blank cutting; gorelocker (SBEM-1250) for elbow fabrication; fitting shaper for transitions and reducers. A complete round duct production cell typically includes the SBTF-1602, flanging machine, and gorelocker operating in parallel.

Electrical Systems Check

All control cabinet wiring, motor connections, and safety interlocks checked per CE electrical safety standard. Documentation included with shipment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the SBTF-1602 produce both Φ80 and Φ1600mm on the same machine?

Yes — the full diameter range Φ80–Φ1600mm is achievable on one machine. Diameter change involves adjusting the forming head and does not require a tooling swap. Larger diameters above Φ1000mm require a wider strip width (180–300mm). Confirm your full diameter range at time of order so the forming head is calibrated for your most common sizes.

What is the actual output at Φ200mm and Φ500mm?

At Φ200mm, 0.6mm GI, 35m/min strip feed: approximately 2,400 linear metres per 8-hour shift under continuous running. At Φ500mm, 0.8mm GI, the forming speed reduces and output is approximately 900–1,100 linear metres per shift. Large-diameter production is slower because each metre of tube requires more strip length per revolution.

How long does a diameter change take?

Diameter changes within a close range (e.g., Φ250 to Φ315mm) take 3–5 minutes. Larger steps (e.g., Φ200 to Φ800mm) take 8–12 minutes including forming head adjustment and test cut. The first cut after a diameter change is always test-measured before batch production begins.

What strip width is needed for each diameter?

Strip width determines the pitch of the helical seam and affects diameter. Typical relationship: Φ200mm uses ~90mm strip, Φ500mm uses ~150mm strip, Φ1000mm uses ~250mm strip. Exact strip widths are calibrated per machine at manufacture — provide your diameter range and SBKJ will specify the correct strip width(s) for your production plan.

Is the flying shear blade a consumable?

The flying shear blade is a semi-consumable — typically lasting 80,000–120,000 cuts under normal GI production conditions. Stainless steel production shortens blade life due to higher material hardness. Two replacement blades are included in the standard spare parts set shipped with every machine. Replacement blades available from the SBKJ parts program with global shipping.

What power supply is needed?

Standard: 380V / 50Hz / 3-phase, 11 kW total installed power. For 60Hz markets (Americas, Philippines, Japan): voltage and frequency conversion is available at the factory — confirm at time of order. Single-phase supply is not suitable for this machine.
