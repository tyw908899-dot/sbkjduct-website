Auto Duct Line SBAL‑V

Fully automatic U‑shape rectangular duct production line. Coil in, finished TDF duct out — with 2–3 operators.

CE Certified ISO 9001 30+ Years Engineering 800–2,500 m²/day Capacity 0.5–1.5 mm Galvanized Steel

Manual duct fabrication caps your factory at 350 m²/day — and ties up 10 workers to get there.

Commercial HVAC contractors who fabricate rectangular duct manually face the same ceiling: output is limited by how fast workers can cut, notch, form, and close each duct section. Labor costs are high, dimensional consistency varies between shifts, and there is no path to 24-hour production.

The SBAL‑V replaces that constraint entirely. Feed coil steel at one end — the line completes Pittsburgh lock forming, TDF or angle flange forming, notching, shearing, and folding automatically. Enter duct dimensions once. The machine runs.

  • Eliminates 6–8 manual operators per shift for rectangular duct production
  • Removes dimensional inconsistency — CNC control holds ±0.5 mm throughout the run
  • Produces TDF flange, angle flange, and drive cleat — all on one line
  • U‑shape layout fits a 14.5 × 5 m workshop footprint
  • Runs 24/7 without shift handover degradation

Common Questions

Questions Buyers Ask Before Ordering an Auto Duct Line

  • Standard lead time for the SBAL‑V is 60–90 days from order confirmation and deposit. Shipping time to most destinations is 15–35 days by sea freight. Electrical requirements are 380V / 50Hz / 3-phase, 40 kW installed capacity with a dedicated circuit breaker. Compressed air at 0.6 MPa is required for the hydraulic unit. We provide a detailed utility specification sheet after order confirmation.
  • Every SBAL‑V carries a 12-month warranty covering manufacturing defects in mechanical and electrical components. Wear items (forming rolls, blades, tooling) are not covered by warranty but are available as original spare parts for the lifetime of the machine. We maintain dedicated stock of all components and ship internationally within 3–5 business days. Remote technical support is available 24/7 via WhatsApp.
  • Yes. The SBAL‑V supports TDF (transverse duct flange) and angle steel flange forming on the same line — switchable via the CNC control panel. Duct size parameters (width, height, length) are entered digitally and stored in memory for recall. The line handles galvanized steel from 0.5 to 1.5 mm thickness. Stainless steel and aluminium configurations are available on request with appropriate tooling changes.
