Taokron at ARBS QLD 2027: Halls 3, Stand 122

Meet the Taokron team at ARBS QLD 2027, the air conditioning, refrigeration and building services exhibition at the Brisbane Convention and Exhibition Centre, 2 and 3 June 2027, 9:00 am to 3:00 pm. Find us in Halls 3, Stand 122.

Book a stand meeting   Ask for a quotation now

Why stop by Stand 122

If your shop makes rectangular or round duct in Queensland, or buys it in and is thinking about making it, this is a chance to talk machines with the people who install them. We supply spiral tubeformers, auto duct lines, TDF flange formers, lockformers, plasma cutters and the support machines around them, delivered door to door, installed and commissioned on site by our own team.

Bring your duct mix, your floor space and your power supply, and we will sketch a machine list with you at the stand.

Real Australian installs

Two recent jobs, with photos and video from site: Kavanagh Industries, Sydney (SBTF-1602 spiral tubeformer, October 2026) and Airtemp, Canberra (8-machine rectangular duct shop, September 2026).
