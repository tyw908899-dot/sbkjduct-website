In these scenarios, customers need tight dimensional tolerance to ensure fast field installation and low air leakage. HVAC Duct Factory Equipment solutions are also used by insulation contractors and fire-rated duct specialists who require repeatable seams and flange profiles before coating or wrapping operations.

FAQ

What thickness range can this equipment process?

Most HVAC duct machinery configurations cover 0.4-1.2 mm galvanized sheet as standard, with optional rollers and tooling for stainless steel and aluminum. Final limits depend on profile type, lock seam geometry, and motor power.

Can one production line make both rectangular and round ducts?

Yes. Many factories combine an auto duct line for rectangular sections with a spiral tubeformer and accessories for round fittings. The mixed layout improves order flexibility for commercial HVAC contracts.

How many operators are needed?

A semi-automatic cell typically runs with 3-5 operators, while a fully integrated line can run with 1-3 people depending on handling automation, stacking, and downstream joining.

Is remote commissioning available?

Yes. Remote setup, training videos, and live diagnostics are common for export projects. On-site commissioning can be arranged for complete duct factory projects.

How do I select machine speed?

Evaluate your target square meter output, shift plan, and common duct gauge. Then match decoiler capacity, forming speed, and cutting cycle time so bottlenecks do not move downstream.

What certifications are relevant for export?

Buyers typically request CE documentation, electrical drawings, and component brand lists. For some regions, additional local compliance and safety labeling may be required before installation.

Can the machine connect with ERP or MES systems?

Many controllers support production data export and job import, enabling barcode-based scheduling and traceability. Integration scope depends on the software architecture selected during project planning.
