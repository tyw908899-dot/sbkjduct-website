Engineering Depth

30 Years of Patent-Protected Production Engineering

01

Proprietary Flying Slitter

Cuts at full strip speed — no stop at the cut cycle. At 35 m/min, stop-start systems lose output at scale.

  • Patented — not available on commodity machines
  • Full-speed cutting eliminates indexed cycle loss
  • Consistent cut geometry across full production run
02

Closed-Loop CNC Control

Servo-driven feedback. The machine self-corrects throughout the run — no operator adjustment needed for drift.

  • ±0.5 mm dimensional tolerance maintained across full runs
  • Self-correcting — no operator intervention for drift
  • Duct size parameters stored in CNC memory
03

Hydraulic Precision Notching

Hydraulic actuation with precise pressure control — consistent corner geometry regardless of tool age.

  • Consistent notch geometry regardless of tool age
  • Hydraulic force control — no cam wear effect
  • Sharp, clean corners on every duct section
04

Multi-Material Tooling Design

GI, stainless, and aluminium on the same machine with documented changeover procedures.

  • Galvanized, stainless, and aluminium supported
  • Documented changeover procedures provided
  • Tooling engineered for material-specific forming forces
