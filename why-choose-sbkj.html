Engineering Support

Engineering Depth Applied to Daily Production Stability

SBKJ has focused on HVAC duct machinery since 1995. The result is practical engineering aimed at repeatable quality and consistent output.

01

Proprietary Flying Slitter

Patented mechanism on the SBTF‑1602 spiral tubeformer. Cuts at full strip speed — no production stop at the cut cycle. Standard indexed cutting systems lose 1–2 seconds per cut; on a 60 m/min strip, that cycle loss is significant at scale.

  • Patented — not available on commodity machines
  • Full-speed cutting eliminates indexed cycle loss
  • Consistent cut geometry across full production run
02

Closed-Loop CNC Control

Every SBKJ auto duct line uses servo-driven length measurement with real-time closed-loop feedback. The machine self-corrects throughout the run. Open-loop systems — common on low-cost alternatives — drift over long runs, requiring manual adjustment and creating scrap.

  • ±0.5 mm dimensional tolerance maintained across full runs
  • Self-correcting — no operator intervention for drift
  • Duct size parameters stored in CNC memory
03

Hydraulic Precision Notching

Rectangular duct line notching on SBKJ machines uses hydraulic actuation with precise pressure control. Cam-driven systems can vary as tools wear, which may affect corner consistency and tool life.

  • Consistent notch geometry regardless of tool age
  • Hydraulic force control — no cam wear effect
  • Sharp, clean corners on every duct section
04

Multi-Material Tooling Design

SBKJ machines are engineered for the material mixes real factories run — galvanized steel, stainless steel, and aluminium on the same machine with documented changeover procedures. Low-cost machines are typically configured for one material only; running other materials without proper tooling causes premature wear and dimensional problems.

  • Galvanized, stainless, and aluminium supported
  • Documented changeover procedures provided
  • Tooling engineered for material-specific forming forces

Automation Depth

Automation That Runs a Factory —
Not Just a Machine

True automation is not a touch screen on an otherwise manual process. SBKJ designs automation that eliminates operator decisions from the production cycle — not just operator effort.

U-shape Factory Layout

U-shape line design reduces workshop footprint, keeps coil input and finished duct output adjacent, and minimizes material handling between processes.

1 entry Operator Interface

Enter duct dimensions once at the CNC panel. The line sequences all forming stages, flange type, notching geometry, and cut length automatically.

24/7 Continuous Operation

SBKJ auto duct lines are designed for continuous multi-shift operation. No shift handover degradation. 2–3 operators per shift, around the clock.

<1.5% Material Waste

CNC-controlled coil feeding and nesting software integration reduces steel waste to under 1.5% — versus 4–7% in manual fabrication environments.
