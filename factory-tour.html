Why visit the SBKJ factory before buying

A six-figure HVAC duct machine purchase is a 10–15 year decision. The cheapest engineering due diligence available is one Friday afternoon at the manufacturer’s factory. You see the production floor, you watch your coil run on the machine you are about to commit to, and you get an indicative landed-cost worksheet you can take into a board meeting the following week. SBKJ has been offering this service since the early 2010s and most buyers who tour the Box Hill North facility commit to a purchase within 90 days.

Three reasons buyers consistently cite for visiting in person rather than relying on PDFs and Zoom calls:

  1. Verification. The factory address, the production capacity, the team size, the QA process — all the items on the 47-point buyer’s checklist — are easy to verify in 90 minutes on the floor.
  2. Coil-specific output. Brochure throughput numbers are tested at ideal conditions. Your specific coil thickness, gauge and supplier may behave differently. Watching your coil run on the machine eliminates the surprise.
  3. Engineering relationship. The engineer who hosts your tour will likely also be your project engineer through FAT, shipping and commissioning. Building that relationship in person before signing accelerates every later decision.

What you’ll see on the production floor

Coil receiving and storage area

Galvanized G275 and G300 coils, stainless 304L and 316L coils, slit width range 137 mm to 1,500 mm sheet, gauge range 0.5 mm to 2.0 mm. Coil traceability via mill certificate (EN 10204 3.1) attached to each coil. Decoiler positions for SBAL-V auto duct line, SBAL-III auto duct line, SBTF spiral tubeformer.

Machining and sub-assembly bay

CNC machining of forming rollers, hydraulic cylinder bodies, gear boxes, structural steel frames. Welded sub-assemblies for cassette holders, fold stations, lockformers and TDF flange roll-forming heads. Mill certificate traceability on all structural steel.

Integration and electrical commissioning bay

Final integration of mechanical sub-assemblies into the complete machine, electrical cabinet wiring (Siemens S7-1500 or Mitsubishi FX5U PLC, Schneider or Allen-Bradley panel components), HMI configuration in the buyer’s preferred language, safety circuit testing per EN ISO 13849-1 PLd, and CE Machinery Directive 2006/42/EC documentation.

FAT (Factory Acceptance Test) bay

Where your machine spends its final 1–2 weeks before shipment. Live coil running with full production cycle, output dimensional verification with traceable instruments, electrical safety testing, EMC test, documented FAT report signed by both SBKJ QA and the buyer’s witness (in-person or via video).

Crating and dispatch yard

Export crating to ISPM-15 fumigated wood standard for North America, Australia, EU and most other markets. Container loading bay with overhead crane and forklift access. Marine packing with humidity indicator, desiccant and shock indicator for sea freight.

Live machine operation against your coil

The single most valuable hour of the tour. We run the machine family you are evaluating against your nominated coil specification — ideally your own 5–10 kg sample sent to Box Hill North 7–10 days before the tour, alternatively a SBKJ-stock coil that matches your spec. You watch the actual cycle time, dimensional output, surface quality and operator interaction. Output samples can be taken home for your QA team to inspect with calipers, micrometers, surface profilometer and any other instruments you use for incoming inspection.

Private engineering consultation

60 minutes in the conference room with the SBKJ engineer who will manage your project. Structured agenda:

  1. Project brief (10 min) — coil specification, throughput target, standards (SMACNA / EN 1505 / AS-NZS 4254 / DW-144), project type, geographical destination.
  2. Machine model match (15 min) — specific SBKJ model recommendation, footprint planning with CAD layout, electrical and compressed air requirements, utility specifications.
  3. Indicative landed cost (15 min) — machine price, ocean freight from Port Melbourne to your nominated port, marine insurance, customs entry estimate (with FTA preferential treatment where applicable: JAEPA, KAFTA, AI-ECTA, AUSFTA), inland trucking and rigging.
  4. FAT and commissioning planning (10 min) — FAT booking timeline, commissioning engineer travel and visa coordination, operator and maintenance training scope, spare parts kit recommendation.
  5. Next steps (10 min) — quotation timeline, contract milestones, payment terms, deposit schedule.

Trade show pre-show machine demonstrations

Virtual machine demonstrations
