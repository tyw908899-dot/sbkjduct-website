Talk to Ryan in Melbourne

Ryan Tao is SBKJ Group's Australia-based sales manager. He handles inquiries, technical questions, factory planning discussions, and project quotes — directly, in your timezone, without automated responses.

How Ryan Can Help You

Ryan's role is not to sell you a machine. It is to help you understand whether a machine is the right fit for your production requirements — and if so, which configuration makes sense for your factory, market, and timeline.

If SBKJ equipment is not the right answer for your situation, Ryan will say so. That is how the relationship stays useful over time.

What Ryan handles:

Machine selection: Understanding your production requirements and matching them to the right model and configuration — not just quoting the most expensive option.
Factory layout review: Reviewing your floor plan and confirming that the machine configuration you are considering fits your available space and workflow.
Import process guidance: Explaining the shipping, customs, and local clearing process for machinery imports to Australia, New Zealand, and Pacific markets.
Formal quotations: Preparing project-specific quotations with machine configuration, pricing, lead time, shipping terms, and warranty details.
Technical support coordination: Connecting you with SBKJ's Jiangyin engineering team for installation, commissioning, and post-delivery technical support.

Useful information to have ready

When you first contact Ryan, the conversation will be more productive if you have thought through:

  • → What type of duct you need to make (rectangular / spiral / both)
  • → Your material (GI / stainless / aluminum) and typical thickness range
  • → Target daily or weekly output (m² or linear meter)
  • → Approximate available floor space (m × m)
  • → Your country and local power supply standard (voltage / Hz)
  • → Your rough timeline for purchasing and installation

Common Questions

What Buyers Usually Ask First

Can I get a price without providing full project details?

A rough price range is possible without full details, but it is not very useful because SBKJ equipment is configured to your specific requirements. The price difference between a basic SBAL-III and a fully configured SBAL-V with stainless tooling is significant. Sharing your material, output target, and duct size range gives Ryan enough to provide a meaningful directional quotation quickly.

Does SBKJ have stock in Australia?

No. SBKJ manufactures in Jiangyin, China and ships direct to buyers worldwide. Machinery is made-to-order configured to your specifications, packed at the factory, and shipped by sea freight. Ryan coordinates the import and clearing process for Australian buyers. Stock machines are not available — this is normal for this category of industrial equipment.

How long does delivery to Australia take?

Factory lead time is typically 45–75 days from order confirmation depending on machine type and current production schedule. Sea freight from Jiangyin port to Australian ports (Melbourne, Sydney, Brisbane) adds approximately 25–35 days. Including clearing and delivery, total time from order confirmation to machine on your floor is typically 3–4 months. Confirm exact lead time with Ryan when you place your order.

Is SBKJ CE certified and can they supply machinery for use in Australia?

Yes, SBKJ machines are CE certified and ISO 9001 certified. CE certification is relevant for European markets. For Australian installations, machinery must comply with WHS (Work Health and Safety) Act requirements and relevant Australian Standards. Ryan can provide CE certificates and documentation to support your compliance process — consult with your local WHS advisor for specific requirements.

What happens if there's a problem after delivery?

SBKJ provides 12-month standard warranty on mechanical and electrical components under normal operating conditions. Post-delivery support is handled via remote diagnosis through video call with the SBKJ technical team. Ryan coordinates communication between you and the Jiangyin team. For major issues, on-site engineer visits can be arranged — confirm terms at time of order.

Is there a minimum order quantity?

No minimum order quantity. Buyers typically order 1–3 machines per inquiry, sometimes a complete production line of 5–8 machines for a greenfield factory setup. Single machine orders are handled the same way as complete line orders.
