## 1) Competitive Teardown

## 1.1 Spiro (spiro.ch) — benchmark for premium industrial trust

### Why they convert (pattern analysis)
- **Swiss engineering framing** is usually immediate and consistent across hero, product architecture, and brand tone.
- **Product clarity** is typically structured by application and output context, not only model names.
- **Institutional trust signals** are generally stronger (legacy, engineering process, references, documentation discipline).
- **Premium restraint in design**: fewer claims, cleaner hierarchy, high confidence language.

### What SBKJ should borrow
- Stronger “system-level” messaging: not machine seller, but production capability partner.
- Cleaner proof hierarchy: evidence > adjectives.
- Better quote path segmentation: “Talk to engineer”, “Get line recommendation”, “Request commercial proposal”.

### What SBKJ can differentiate on
- Faster responsiveness and customization cycle **[VERIFY: typical engineering response time]**.
- Better value-per-output vs premium European capex, framed with payback math.
- Broader line-integration flexibility across budget tiers.

---

## 1.2 Sente Makina (sentemakina.com.tr) — benchmark for breadth + exporter clarity

### Why they convert (pattern analysis)
- **Broad catalog depth** in a manufacturing-first tone.
- **Exporter credibility** often supported by global supply language and product range density.
- **Practical buying signals**: specs, category navigation, and conversion accessibility.

### Where they may be weaker (opportunity for SBKJ)
- Commodity feel risk when many products are presented without a strong strategic buying narrative.
- Limited differentiation narrative if everyone says “quality + service + export”.

### SBKJ response strategy
- Keep product breadth, but introduce authority stack:
  1) outcomes,
  2) engineering proof,
  3) service reliability,
  4) commercial certainty.

## 2) Current Site Audit (sbkjduct.com repo snapshot)

## 2.1 Homepage messaging

### Strengths
- Strong ambition and category relevance in hero copy.
- Clear machine family mention (spiral, auto line, fittings).
- Existing ROI-oriented section direction is strategically correct.

### Gaps
- Several hard claims appear without visible proof anchors on-page:
  - 5000+ installs, 60+ patents, warranty specifics, tolerance figures, support windows.
- “Authority proof” and “conversion CTA architecture” are mixed together; decision-stage users need cleaner paths.
- Heavy claim density can trigger skepticism for high-ticket B2B buyers if source proof is not attached.

### Fix priority
- Keep strong tone, but convert all high-impact claims into **proof-backed blocks** with references or **[VERIFY]** placeholders until validated.

---

## 2.2 Product persuasion quality

### Strengths
- Product coverage is broad.
- Featured machine selection aligns with core demand categories.

### Gaps
- Many pages are still model/spec centric rather than buyer-outcome centric.
- Inconsistent page depth across products.
- Limited standardized “commercial confidence” components (delivery scope, installation, spare parts, training, acceptance criteria).

### Fix priority
- Standardize a high-conversion product template (see Section 4).

---

## 2.3 Brand clarity

### Strengths
- Positioning toward full-line manufacturing partner is present.

### Gaps
- Brand voice occasionally drifts toward exporter brochure style.
- Premium industrial hierarchy can be improved with tighter information layering and fewer unverified superlatives.

---

## 2.4 Differentiation clarity

### Current state
- Differentiation claims exist but are not consistently framed against buyer alternatives:
  - premium Europe,
  - local low-cost factories,
  - in-house manual production.

### Required shift
- Add explicit “decision frameworks” and comparison structures:
  - **Total cost of ownership**,
  - **output per labor hour**,
  - **risk of downtime/rework**,
  - **service continuity**.

---

## 2.5 Conversion path audit

### Strengths
- Contact pathways exist.
- WhatsApp/quick contact presence supports fast response.

### Gaps
- Not enough stage-specific conversion options:
  - Early stage: “Get line recommendation”.
  - Mid stage: “Send your duct mix for ROI estimate”.
  - Late stage: “Request formal quotation + delivery window”.
- No obvious progressive qualification flow capturing:
  - country,
  - monthly output target,
  - material thickness range,
  - labor baseline,
  - project deadline.

### Fix priority
- Build a guided “Talk to Engineer” funnel with low friction first step.

## 4) Reusable Product Page Template (ROI + Trust Optimized)

## Section order (required)
1. **Hero (decision context + primary CTA)**
   - Headline: machine + core outcome.

   - CTA 2: Get Technical Datasheet.

2. **Fit & use-case quick filter**
   - Best for (shop type / output range / material range).
   - Not ideal for (disqualify poor-fit buyers).

3. **Commercial outcomes block**
   - Output range (with test condition note).
   - Labor impact.
   - Scrap/rework impact.
   - Payback guidance.
   - Use placeholders if proof pending: **[VERIFY: ROI baseline assumptions]**.

4. **Technical proof block**
   - Key mechanisms and why they matter.
   - Tolerance/precision claims with evidence notes.
   - Certification and compliance references **[VERIFY: cert IDs]**.

   - Lead time, installation requirements, operator count, warranty, shipping, payment terms.
